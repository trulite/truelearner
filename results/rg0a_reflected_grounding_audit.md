## Downward-path opacity

The integrated hot path receives only the immutable lower machine, the frozen
anonymous role learner, the frozen learned program, and a dense temporary
binding table produced by signature matching. At a route-source cell it:

1. reads the cell's temporary role;
2. evaluates the frozen learned arrows from that anonymous role;
3. fires the selected learned arrow;
4. reads the target role's opaque lower location;
5. enqueues an ordinary spike at that location.

It does not construct `ProgramChoices`, call the frozen direct executor,
compare a `LowerRole`, resolve an evaluator target, invoke an operation
callback, or receive an answer. Semantic `LowerRole` data is confined to lower
cell construction, scoring, CONCRETE, development fixtures, and ORACLE.
