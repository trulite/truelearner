## Why the two authorized additions were insufficient

An anonymous event bus can order and route spikes. A continuous scheduler can
advance them to a work bound and resume exactly. Neither operation determines
how a spike becomes a correspondence motif, learned role, causal link, event
observation, encounter snapshot, or credit history.

Three candidate completions were rejected by the protocol:

1. dedicated one-hot mechanism/role ports would supply a representation of the
   very roles the organism is meant to learn;
2. timing/phase encodings would choose a new representational convention and
   require new decoders;
3. direct struct-to-struct function adapters would reproduce the hidden
   experiment scheduler outside CELL/ARROW/SPIKE physics.

Thus the missing work is not merely transport or scheduling. The mechanisms
must first be physically realized on a common substrate, or a new lawful
representation-learning layer must be introduced. Both exceed I0's allowed
integration physics.
