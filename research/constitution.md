# Research Constitution

```text
Locate -> change minimally -> observe participation
   -> preserve lifetime boundaries -> integrate cleanly
```

## Five laws

1. Locate the first missing physical transition.
2. Change the smallest thing capable of producing that transition.
3. Let actual participation create the state needed for later consequence.
4. Keep learning, execution, and lifetime physically distinct.
5. Adopt nothing until clean integration succeeds without hidden authority.

## Discovery

- Parallelize localization while the first divergence is unknown.
- Once localized, batch competing minimal solves and necessary composition arms.
- State predictions and killing falsifiers before execution.
- Kill bad ideas with tiny fixtures before spending a full evidence run.
- Reuse isolated E2B workers for development speed and retain every negative result.
- Force convergence after each declared round.

Discovery produces development evidence. Survival means only "not falsified within
the declared budget."

## Authority

- Freeze the protocol and exact candidate before execution.
- Use a fresh one-shot sandbox with no discovery state.
- Preserve unchanged positive references and negative controls.
- Distinguish infrastructure failure, negative result, and inconclusive evidence.
- Require explicit adjudication and authorization before promotion.

## Integrity

- Do not reconstruct causal history when actual participation can record it.
- Do not patch failed mechanisms or negative controls.
- Do not let a stale benchmark contract dictate physical behavior.
- Require reference, replay, Production, and natural-quiescence integrity where applicable.
- Reject semantic IDs, correct-answer knowledge, episode IDs, path memory, adaptive curricula,
  or evaluator-side intelligence unless explicitly under test.
- Preserve a clean pre-frontier checkpoint before adopting new physics.

## Coupling boundary

Research publishes neutral protocols and imports neutral evidence. It does not invoke
an implementation factory, interpret a factory receipt as scientific evidence, or
allow an external producer to mutate research state.
