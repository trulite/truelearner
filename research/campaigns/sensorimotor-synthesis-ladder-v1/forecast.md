Stop dependent rungs at the first failure, but retain isolated diagnostic fixtures that
do not encode their claimed result.

## Failure descent

For the first failing rung, inspect in order: external incidence, local path proposal,
path traversal, candidate admission, motor integration, output, physical world change,
return opening, consequence delivery, consolidation, and later traversal. A complete
trace through one transition kills it as the frontier and moves diagnosis to the next.
No removal result is admissible while rung 8 has not passed.

## Conditional downward ablation

Freeze the exact rung-8 survivor. Remove and restore, one at a time: reverse
consolidation, current-arrival motor opportunity, recent consequence preference,
cooperating consequence cohort, origin sparsity, finite return, local opportunity
replacement, structural locality, and indexed aging. Rerun rungs 0-8 after every
removal. Then test only evidence-motivated pairs whose individual removals survive.
A law is redundant only when behavior, controls, exact replay, natural quiescence,
formed-path equality, and cost bounds all survive.

## Post-convergence frontier

If the ascent fails, the earliest traced discontinuity is the only admissible solve
frontier. If the ascent succeeds, the smallest surviving ablation failure set becomes
the necessity frontier. Neither discovery outcome changes default organism authority.
