# Sensorimotor dependency sweep forecast

The sweep tests every forecasted transition independently. “Supplied” means a
neutral physical fixture establishes the prerequisite without encoding the measured
result. Failure falsifies current sufficiency only.

| Arm | Transition | Status before run | Supplied prerequisite | Killing observation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| causal-local-credit | one consequence strengthens only its participating output path | constrained | two simultaneous causal paths | wrong or multiple paths strengthen |
| delayed-return-lifetime | a participating path remains creditable across physical delay | established | one used path and no competitor | delayed consequence cannot return exactly |
| local-alternative-exposure | an unanswered local route yields once to a fresh neighbor | constrained | one consequential reuse followed by no outcome | no single fresh neighbor appears |
| displaced-return-closure | replacement retires only the displaced unanswered return | constrained | one fresh local opportunity | stale return remains or fresh return closes |
| consequence-supported-continuation | one real consequence keeps the fresh route eligible for bounded reuse | predicted | exact alternative exposure and local consequence | fresh route immediately yields back |
| no-consequence-release | a locally dominant route releases after participation without consequence | predicted | physically strengthened route plus fresh competitor | dominant route locks or unrelated route changes |
| reversible-local-competition | later contrary consequence can reverse a prior local strength advantage | predicted | separately trained opposite paths | earlier winner cannot be displaced |
| sparse-shared-opportunity | one shared opportunity activates bounded distant motor structure | predicted | anonymous shared root and distant ready outputs | activation and work grow with every distant output |
| coalition-separate-credit | simultaneous outputs retain separate causal credit | predicted | one joint physical event with two local outcomes | either consequence credits the other path |
| delayed-multisurface-alignment | one action can retain distinct delayed consequences from several physical surfaces | predicted | two anonymous consequence sources | the topology cannot represent both or credits them indistinguishably |
| composed-output-sequence | a learned consequence can physically invoke the next learned output | predicted | two locally trained stages and a world transition between them | recall stops after the first output |
| scale-selectivity | active selection stays bounded as dormant body surface grows | predicted | identical shared opportunity at two scales | output, active frontier, and work scale with all available outputs |
| consolidated-reuse | a learned local route remains executable and isolated inside a larger body | predicted | many independently trained local routes | recalling one route fires others or loses the learned route |
| one-joint-composition | surviving local laws compose into bidirectional recovery | predicted composition | frozen replacement protocol and reflected joint world | either limit cannot be reached and left |

After the batch converges, the earliest falsified transition with surviving
prerequisites becomes the next solve frontier. End-to-end body stages remain gated;
the isolated probes are not.
