## Complete candidate

The candidate is one organism capable of recursively constructing bounded instances
of the same learner physics. There is no privileged root type: every child receives
the same construction capability and may become a parent when its own participation
discovers another causal closure. Construction is not a semantic action primitive: a
learner may arise only from an observed physical round trip in which an actual output
is followed by bounded proprioceptive change and consequence returns through the
participating structure. Ordinary physical resources bound realized depth; no
evaluator supplies a maximum nesting level.

## Failure descent

At the first failed rung, trace in this order: output participation, physical change,
proprioceptive incidence, local return, closure evidence, construction event, child
boundary membership, inherited construction capability, descendant construction,
child path formation, child output, each boundary-crossing return, adjacent-parent
candidate admission, and later recruitment. A complete trace through one step moves
the frontier to the next. No removal ablation is admissible before rung 10 passes.

The strongest inherited counterexample is the complete monolithic synthesis: it moved
on twelve of sixteen one-joint steps in both directions yet reached neither reflected

necessary. A child must beat that exact reference without extra semantic information

## Conditional downward ablation ladder

Freeze the exact rung-10 survivor and restore it between removals. Remove, one at a
time, causal-closure discovery, first-level construction, inherited construction
capability, local consequence, nested consequence continuity, adjacent-parent
recruitment, finite return lifetime, reverse execution, origin sparsity, structural
locality, and active-frontier indexing. Rerun rungs 0-10 after every removal. Then test
only evidence-motivated pairs whose individual removals survive. A mechanism is
redundant only when capability, causal controls, depth symmetry, reflection, transfer,
exact replay, natural quiescence, and cost bounds all remain intact.

## First campaign and post-convergence frontier

The first campaign should freeze the complete candidate and batch rungs 0-10, the
matched monolithic reference, false-closure controls, per-transition trace fixtures,
and conditional ablations. The earliest uncertain transition is rung 2: construction
of a child from causal closure. If it fails, the next frontier is the first missing
physical event between closure evidence and bounded child existence. If the complete
candidate succeeds, the first necessary mechanism exposed by downward ablation becomes
the next frontier. Neither outcome changes default organism authority.
