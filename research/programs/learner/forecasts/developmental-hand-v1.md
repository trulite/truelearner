## Why this ladder

The hand is the developmental target, but the learner is never told that it has a
hand. The world exposes only anonymous physical incidence and consequences. Each
rung adds one physical dependency that the previous rung did not require. This makes
a failure informative: it locates missing learner physics instead of merely lowering
a hand score.

The inherited monolithic candidate already crossed the first-output boundary. It
changed the reflected joint on twelve of sixteen steps and used both signs, yet
reached neither limit. The current frontier is therefore cumulative, reversible
closed-loop control—not motor emission. The recursive candidate adds fresh causal
owners, current same-owner proprioception, and private recent-consequence memory;
the first campaign asks whether those ingredients close the old loop before adding
another joint.

## Upward capability ladder

| Rung | Added physical requirement | Required observation | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Integrity | None | Frozen references replay and quiesce; local cost remains bounded. | Any inherited control changes. |
| 1. One anonymous joint | Cumulative reversible control | Reach and leave both reflected limits, reverse under reflection, and recover from a mid-run perturbation without evaluator termination. | Either limit, escape, reversal, or recovery is absent. |
| 2. One two-joint finger | Two local loops in serial kinematics | Each joint develops both directions independently; distal effect remains learnable when proximal state changes. | One joint monopolizes credit, development depends on fixed order, or serial composition loses rung 1. |
| 3. Two opposing digits | Independent siblings plus contact | Each digit retains local control; contact consequence recruits opposition without cross-sibling preference leakage. | Cross-credit, broad coactivation, or inability to form/release opposition. |
| 4. Coordinated hand | Reusable coalitions and sequences | Multiple digits form, release, and reuse more than one grasp under changed object position and reflection. | A single frozen posture, semantic grasp identity, or evaluator-provided sequence is required. |
| 5. Morphology and task transfer | Changed scale, ordering, and consequences | The same physics develops on reflected, reordered, and rescaled hands and transfers prior local competence without copied answers. | Success depends on one morphology, curriculum order, or evaluator-selected target. |

Every later rung is blocked by the first actual prerequisite failure. Isolated
diagnostics may expose only the anonymous physical inputs required to observe their
transition; they may not provide the missing competence.

## First-failure descent

At a failed rung, compare the complete candidate with its frozen negative reference
and locate the earliest divergence in this order:

1. external incidence and physical opportunity;
2. local path proposal and traversal;
3. candidate admission under current proprioception;
4. motor integration and output;
5. world change, reflection, and contact;
6. return opening and participating consequence delivery;
7. deepest causal-owner resolution and private write;
8. later owner-local read, continuation, reversal, and release;
9. adjacent-parent recruitment;
10. sibling isolation, sequence, and coalition reuse.

A complete trace through one transition moves the frontier to the next. A high-level
label such as “bad grasp” or “oscillation” is not a mechanism diagnosis.

## Conditional hypothesis-fit map

This table is a preregistered routing policy for successor campaigns, not permission
to alter a running arm.

| First broken transition | Previously justified candidate family | Discriminating requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Path traverses but no physical output occurs | Current-arrival opportunity and signed motor integration | Exact timing change restores output while shifted incidence remains negative. |
| Both signs occur but travel repeatedly reverses before a limit | Owner-local recent consequence, continuation, competition, and bounded route lifetime | Recent participating success extends cumulative travel without permanent lock or loss of the opposite sign. |
| A limit is reached but cannot be left | Consequence release, current context, and strength-conditioned renewal | Withheld consequence releases the incumbent and reflection permits escape without evaluator reset. |
| Participating consequence occurs but no private record changes | Finite return opening, deepest-owner resolution, and live-generation write | Only the accepted participating live generation writes; stale, duplicate, and unrelated returns remain negative. |
| Private write exists but later choice ignores it | Exact owner-local read under current same-owner proprioception | The matching owner changes preference; parent and siblings remain neutral. |
| Each joint works alone but serial or sibling composition fails | Structural locality, sibling isolation, adjacent-parent recruitment, and nested consequence | Composition preserves each primitive control and attributes consequence only through actual participation. |
| Opposition works once but cannot form multiple grasps | Learned sequence and coalition participation with bounded release | More than one coalition is reusable; either-alone, unrelated-member, and stale controls stay negative. |

If the localized wall matches no existing family, preserve the counterexample and
forecast a new physical law before writing a solve arm. Benchmark progress alone is
never a reason to admit a mechanism.

## Downward ablation after success

No removal ablation is admissible before the complete candidate passes the current
rung. Freeze the survivor, remove one mechanism, rerun every rung up to that point,
then restore the survivor. Later hand success should ablate recursive construction,
owner-local consequence, current proprioceptive gating, finite return, reverse
execution, structural locality, sibling isolation, nested consequence, and coalition
formation individually before testing justified pairs. A mechanism is necessary only
if its removal breaks the upward ladder while controls, replay, quiescence, and costs
remain interpretable.

## Immediate campaign

`developmental-hand-one-joint-v1` remains an unexecuted frozen record of the original
bundled hypothesis. Its implementation preflight exposed a confound: truthful
anonymous physical origins changed the trajectory before recursive ownership could
be interpreted, so improvement could not be attributed to recursive memory.

`developmental-hand-proprioception-decomposition-v1` is the admissible successor. It
crosses synthetic versus truthful physical provenance with monolithic versus recursive
learner physics, runs the complete truthful-recursive candidate first, and measures
trajectory plus topology and runtime cost. Only the factorial comparison may attribute
an effect to truthful proprioception, recursive owner memory, or their interaction.
Finger and hand rungs remain stopped until this successor converges.
