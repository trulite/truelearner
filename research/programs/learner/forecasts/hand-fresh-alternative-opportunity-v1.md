## Upward ladder and failure descent

The candidate already preserves truthful transition admission, replay, natural
quiescence, and four real position changes. It fails at the next transition: after
unchanged samples stop renewing consequence, the incumbent output remains
executable while the neighboring output has complete path inputs but never becomes
executable. Later hand rungs remain stopped.

The first uncertain dependency is therefore the transformation from live signed
path drive to an executable fresh candidate before ordinary output ranking.

## Conditional solve forecast

Only if the first explanation survives, test whether one live unanswered return on
an executable incumbent supplies one transient opportunity to one physically local
fresh candidate before algebraic cancellation. The opportunity may select between
already present opposing signs; it may not add drive, create a route, name a motor,
or persist after the return is answered or superseded.

Required controls are answered return, no alternative, nonlocal output, owner
incompatibility, repeated observation of the same unanswered return, reflection,
exact replay, natural quiescence, and bounded work. The strongest inherited
counterexample is the origin-factorization runaway: global origin-scoped release
can create a non-quiescent motor loop, so locality and one-effect lifetime are
mandatory.

No downward ablation is admissible until a complete candidate reaches and leaves
both reflected limits and recovers from perturbation. The post-convergence frontier
is whichever of route strength, ownership, or pre-executability opportunity is
first directly observed to fail.
