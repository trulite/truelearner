# CORE1-E13 — history composition protocol v2 amendment

## Status

Development amendment only. Protocol v1 remains frozen and no definitive E13
history-composition evidence has been spent.

## Preflight mismatch

The v1 preflight correctly resolved the first context, but each direct
transient input at a generated contact was also an ordinary local-variation
opportunity. The resulting unsupported weak structures lawfully occupied the
bounded arena before later contexts could create their own contact alternatives.

This is neither a learning failure nor a reason to suppress variation.

## Sole schedule amendment

Separate consequence admission from the following context prime. After the
previous action receives its ordinary world-change consequence, advance a
fixed `12` physical ticks before priming the next context. Existing phase-free
local forgetting removes unsupported exploratory debris. Consequence-supported
material is allowed to survive by its already-established resistance law.

Apply the same fixed gap after the final consequence and before fresh probes.
The gap is identical for every context, profile, sign, output, and identity. It
is not selected from behavior and does not change the history pair.

## Everything else unchanged

All v1 profiles, worlds, actions, history order, early/late timing, learning
law, physical runtime, prohibitions, comparisons, and acceptance predicates
remain exact. No ARC execution is authorized.
