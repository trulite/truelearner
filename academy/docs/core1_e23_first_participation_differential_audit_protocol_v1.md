## Stage predicates

Record pre-admission and post-admission diagnostics for action route 1 and the
physical transition trace of the compared action admission.

1. **Context present** — the observation resolves the frozen frame to the
   expected context and the trace contains a physical firing of its candidate
   source or context-trace CELL.
2. **Contact live** — at least one live generated contact has both a source
   stem and an outgoing link to the motor after context establishment. Record
   whether the pair pre-existed the action admission or was proposed during it.
3. **Route eligible at perturbation** — at least one live contact pair has
   positive stem and outgoing material before the babbler fires. A pair may
   pre-exist or be completed by earlier physical proposals in the same trace.
4. **Drive emitted** — a stem or outgoing route arrow records increased local
   participation during the action admission.
5. **Route participates** — the ordinary observation contains a physical motor
   crossing/action.

Also record source, context-trace, babbler, contact and motor firing events;
proposal times; live stem/outgoing counts and materials; route participation
deltas; outward crossings; PhysicalWork; ticks; and natural quiescence.
