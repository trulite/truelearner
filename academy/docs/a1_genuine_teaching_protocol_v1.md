# A1 Genuine Teaching + A1-V Replay — Development Protocol v1

Status: preregistered before implementation or behavioral execution.

This protocol advances Academy development only. It does not change or advance
TrueLearner physical-law authority. R7 remains out of scope.

## Claim

Academy can create a generated physical teaching world in which repeated,
consequence-supported co-participation matures one weak joint route. A later
fresh probe can exercise that route without teaching it. Academy evaluates the
result outside TrueLearner and records enough physical evidence to reproduce
the experience exactly.

The first claim is deliberately narrower than free-form naming or question
answering. It establishes genuine acquired relation recognition. It does not
claim arbitrary symbol retrieval, visual invariance, transfer, or mastery.

## Scientific boundary

Academy may know:

- which generated relation is being taught;
- which external consequence is delivered;
- which outward crossing constitutes probe success;
- the capability and control represented by a row.

TrueLearner receives only ordinary `CellSpec`, `ArrowSpec`, and `SpikeInput`
physics. It never receives a capability identifier, expected answer, pass/fail
value, reward bit, relation identifier, mode, or evaluator result.

No general Probe may compare submitted input with itself. The legacy
`InteractionRequest.expected_text` path must be removed from scientific
capability evidence. Echo may exist only as an explicitly named negative or
copying control.

## Generated physical world

Each seed creates fresh physical identities, fresh anonymous origins, and one
of the preregistered layout transformations (normal, reflected, or allocation
reversed). The world contains:

- primitive physical participants `A`, `B`, `C`, and `D`;
- one unit participation trace per primitive traversal;
- symmetric weak joint opportunities for `A+B` and `A+C`;
- downstream effect paths;
- one outward boundary;
- an ordinary returned physical participant;
- qualified LR-C Modulatory paths back to recently eligible joint structure.

All joint candidate arrows begin with resistance 1. Nothing is selected by a
semantic branch. `A+B` is selected only because it physically traverses and
receives completed-world Modulatory support. The unsupported `A+C` opportunity
experiences the same availability and pressure but no qualifying consequence.

Teaching uses two completed `A+B` loops separated by the existing physical
schedule. Probe inputs never deliver modulation. Ordinary Drive arriving while
structure is eligible cannot strengthen it.

## Development ladder

### PROBE — one real learned relation

One seed must establish:

1. Before teaching, the weak `A+B` route is not reusable after pressure.
2. Two completed `A+B` loops produce qualified Modulatory updates.
3. After pressure, the supported route remains live.
4. A fresh, non-teaching `A+B` probe produces exactly one expected outward
   crossing and naturally quiesces.
5. The same probe replay is exact from its recorded before-checkpoint.

If PROBE fails, stop. Do not repair it in UI code or evaluator logic.

### MICRO — causal controls

For each seed:

- `A` alone (echo control): zero recognized relation crossings;
- repeated adjacent `A+B` without returned consequence: zero maturation;
- `A+C` distractor: zero recognized relation crossings after pressure;
- `B+C` wrong context: zero recognized relation crossings;
- modulation without prior joint traversal: zero plasticity updates;
- completed `A+B` teaching: positive joint-route update;
- fresh `A+B` probe: exactly one crossing and zero plasticity updates;
- probe mode: no Academy-triggered teaching or modulation;
- all rows: bounded work, exact replay, natural quiescence.

### GATE — generated identities and physical variants

Run disjoint seeds across normal, reflected, and reverse-allocation layouts.
The behavior must follow physical participation, not physical ID, allocation
order, origin, or layout direction. At least one text-admitted case and one
raster-admitted case must pass their genuine learned-relation probe and their
matched negatives. Raster admission must derive spikes from raster bytes or
their fixed physical sample geometry; no evaluator label may be admitted as a
spike.

## Capability evidence

Only the generated A1 relation-recognition capability receives A1 evidence.
Legacy generic interaction/copy capabilities do not receive credit from these
rows. A status transition must cite the exact `ExperienceRecord` identifiers
that caused it.

The first positive A1 result may mark the capability `EMERGING`; stronger
mastery labels remain reserved for later acquisition, transfer, and retention
programs.

## A1-V ExperienceRecord

Every Teach, Probe, Transfer, and Retention execution records:

- stable experience ID and generator seed;
- Academy mode, capability, probe family, and evaluator outcome;
- canonical before and after live checkpoints or immutable content refs;
- before and after body fingerprints;
- physical clock range and pressure phase;
- complete admitted `SpikeInput` stream;
- organism-visible raster before/after or immutable refs;
- shared-world raster before/after or immutable refs;
- complete outward crossings;
- `PhysicalWork`, resident bytes, and quiescence;
- deterministic replay result;
- observer-only annotations, kept outside organism-visible pixels.

Replay is selected by experience ID, not implicitly "latest". Physical replay
restores the recorded before-checkpoint, admits the recorded spikes, and
compares crossings, body, clock, work, and quiescence. Observer rendering and
Academy re-evaluation are separately named operations.

## Durable evidence

The existing private, versioned Academy S3 content store is used as an
immutable evidence sink. Checkpoints, physical inputs, surfaces, and episode
manifests are stored by SHA-256. An episode manifest refers to its immutable
blobs and contains no AWS credentials.

Local execution must remain possible when S3 is unavailable. In that case the
complete record remains replayable in memory and publication has an explicit
pending/error state; storage failure cannot change organism physics or the
scientific result.

## Playground handoff

Only after PROBE, MICRO, and GATE pass:

- Teach launches a generated teaching case;
- Probe launches an independently generated non-teaching probe;
- History is a selectable filmstrip of persisted experiences;
- selecting an item replays that exact historical experience;
- organism, shared-world, and observer views derive from the same record;
- no submitted text is silently reused as expected output.

The existing visual direction and interaction layout remain unchanged except
where needed to expose genuine Teach/Probe state and selected replay.

## Acceptance and stop condition

A1/A1-V development is positive only if all preregistered functional,
negative, replay, separation, and storage-integrity clauses pass. TrueLearner
source and physical laws must remain unchanged.

After a positive A1/A1-V handoff, stop for human review. Do not start A2,
R7, curriculum scheduling, retention scheduling, shared-world drawing, or
transport automatically.
