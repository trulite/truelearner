# CORE1-E21 — Temporary Physical Credit Return Protocol v1

## Status

Preregistered before any E21 implementation or runtime observation. E21 is one
narrow broken-wire test rooted at frozen E20 result `fda9bf1`. It is not a
tournament and does not advance accepted physics or ARC authority.

## Question

Can a payload-free temporary physical return connection place a used local
route on the later consequence propagation graph, so unchanged PQLC can credit
it after forward completion?

```text
route participates
-> temporary physical credit-return connection forms
-> forward action completes
-> return connection survives
-> consequence physically traverses it
-> used contact receives Modulatory incidence
-> existing PQLC updates participating route material
-> return connection disappears
```

## Frozen E20 base

Retain unchanged:

- E20's frame, body, opaque permutations, useful assignments, two supported
  opportunities, autonomous probe, and bounded four-completion budget;
- OPEN/CLOSED junction lifetime and positive completion receptor;
- the completion-rooted two-stage fresh local variation cycle;

- E20's CLOSED, missing-completion, post-success, replay, and mechanics gates;
- E18 in-flight and all-live-arrow protection disabled.

No PQLC gain, propagation, or material-update rule may change.

## Sole candidate — temporary physical credit return

When an actual local contact route physically participates, its transient
traversal capture is converted immediately at quiescence into an ordinary
physical Modulatory connection:

```text
existing consequence-returning CELL -> actually used contact CELL
```

The connection uses ordinary `SourceFires` transmission. It contains no action,
motor, route, path, context, episode, attempt, reward, outcome, timer, deadline,
or TTL payload. Its physical endpoints are its entire meaning.

Traversal capture may be used only inside the same physical admission to find
the contact whose stem and outgoing route arrows actually traversed. Capture
bits must be clear before completion begins and may not persist as E20
USED-PENDING state.

While the connection exists, the contact and its incident participating Drive
arrows are structurally part of the temporary return topology and may not be
deallocated. Activation and participation still relax normally. No unrelated
route, contact, or alternative receives protection.

Completion cannot traverse, remove, credit, or create the connection. A later
ordinary consequence fires the existing returning CELL; the consequence spike
must physically traverse the temporary connection and arrive Modulatorily at
the used contact. Existing local-return/PQLC then decides whether any material
updates occur.

After the admitted consequence transition finishes, all temporary credit-return
connections in that local OPEN interaction are physically removed and OPEN
closes. If no consequence arrives, the connection remains at quiescence; it
does not expire from time, silence, completion count, or another OPEN wake.

Multiple actually used contacts may have multiple simultaneous return
connections. E21 stores no episode identity capable of assigning a later
consequence to only one of them. The eight-seed matrix must expose rather than
repair any resulting credit ambiguity.

## Decisive P2-first gate

Before rerunning P1 or any matrix row, run corrected useful-first seed 0 in
Reference, exact Reference replay, and Production. In both fixed opportunities
require:

- useful route 1 physically participates;
- at least one temporary return connection forms at its used contact;
- E20 passive pending count is zero before completion;
- completion is positively delivered before consequence;
- the same connection remains after completion;
- ordinary consequence is admitted;
- consequence records `Modulatory deliveries > 0`;
- unchanged PQLC records `plasticity updates > 0`;
- all temporary return connections disappear after consequence;
- OPEN changes to CLOSED only after consequence;
- route 1 is autonomous after the second consequence;
- work is bounded, activity naturally quiesces, replay is exact, and Reference
  equals Production.

Any failure stops E21. No repair, P1 rerun, evidence marker, or matrix is
permitted inside E21 after a valid P2 failure.

## P1 retention gate

Only after P2 passes, rerun hard seed 7. Require E20 P1 unchanged:

- opaque route sequence reaches useful route 4 within four completions;
- every retry follows a delivered completion and exactly one fresh variation
  cycle;
- no timeout or unreturned OPEN loop;
- all activity naturally quiesces;
- exact replay and Reference/Production equality.

Temporary return connections may accumulate only on contacts that actually
participated. P1 must not rely on their semantic identity.

## Eight-seed gate

Only after P2 and P1 both pass, emit the one-shot evidence marker and execute
the frozen eight seeds. For every seed require:

- useful route participates within four positive completions in both
  opportunities;
- every participating contact creates a physical return connection;
- useful consequence physically traverses at least one such connection;
- both useful consequences produce nonzero Modulatory deliveries and PQLC
  updates;
- all return connections disappear and OPEN closes after consequence;
- the useful route is autonomous after two consequences;
- mandatory controls, bounded work, natural quiescence, exact replay, and
  Reference/Production equality pass.

Primary success is `8/8`; partial coverage earns nothing.

## Mandatory controls

- CLOSED plus ordinary silence: no action and no return connection.
- OPEN action without completion: exactly one attempt; connection may remain,
  but no retry and no credit occurs.
- Completion while CLOSED: delivered, zero variation cycles, zero return-path
  traversal, zero learning.
- Completion while OPEN: does not change connection count or PQLC material.
- Consequence with no participating route: zero temporary connection and zero
  E21 credit.
- Post-success completion: delivered but ineligible, with no connection or
  variation cycle.
- E20 USED-PENDING bits: zero throughout every interval after route admission.

## Representation audit and rejection screen

Reject E21 if:

- the temporary fact is only a passive bit or table entry;
- consequence cannot be observed traversing an actual connection;
- an untraversed contact receives a connection;
- the connection stores supplied identity or semantic payload;
- a connection appears from completion, silence, timeout, or evaluator
  usefulness lookup;
- the connection directly changes durable material rather than delivering an
  ordinary Modulatory event to existing PQLC;
- route material is globally protected;
- any connection disappears without admitted consequence/reset;
- successful consequence leaves a connection or OPEN live;
- replay, mechanics equality, bounded work, or quiescence fails.

## Allowed implementation surface

E21 may add only experimental `core1` support for:

1. tagging an ordinary Modulatory arrow as a temporary credit-return
   connection;
2. topology-derived structural retention of that connection's target contact
   and its incident participating Drive arrows;
3. consequence-triggered removal and diagnostic counts;
4. Academy conversion of same-admission traversal capture into the physical
   connection, followed immediately by capture-bit clearing.

No accepted default, durable checkpoint schema, PQLC rule, consequence gain,
ARC policy, or prior frozen evaluator may change.
