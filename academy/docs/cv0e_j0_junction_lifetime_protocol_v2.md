## Boundary fixture

links before the first admitted arrival:

```text
Anchor -> P -> generated candidate topology -> X -> Anchor
```

The anchor CELL and both anchor links have high resistance sufficient for the
complete experiment. They are identical across Reference and Production and
across the signed alternatives. They are not generated by variation and carry
no selected sign, role, or evaluator answer.

The anchor does not fire in the experiment. `Anchor -> P` therefore does not
traverse. The tested candidate impulses do not make X fire, so `X -> Anchor`
does not traverse. The anchor topology makes P, X, and the anchor itself
non-orphan without producing the tested action or consequence.
