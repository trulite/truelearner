# CC0 consequence-supported CELL consolidation protocol v1

Status: frozen before any CC0 organism, evaluator, or executable change.

Parent: CL0 Gate-9 negative `a20a6b8`.

## Question

Can the same ordinary local consequence that makes an actually participating
ARROW persist also make an actually participating ordinary CELL persist
longer, without introducing a contact-specific CELL class or semantic role?

CL0 Gates 1--8 established phase-free ordinary CELL decay, generation-safe
death, stale-reference safety, and resident-slot reuse. CC0 tests only the
missing consolidation path identified by CL0 Gate 9.

## Sole candidate law

Every ordinary live CELL gains the same continuously relaxing local
participation quantity already established for ordinary ARROW participation:

```text
actual ordinary CELL firing
    -> CELL-local participation rises

ordinary elapsed time
    -> CELL-local participation relaxes continuously

qualified Modulatory arrival at that same CELL
    + remaining CELL participation
    -> CELL resistance increases by the same local consequence-strength law
       used for ordinary durable ARROW structure
    -> CELL-local durable decay load rebases because the durable structure
       itself changed
```

Drive delivery that does not make the CELL fire is not CELL participation.
Firing or repeated use alone cannot increase CELL resistance. Modulation does
not consume participation, fire the CELL, inspect incident topology, or infer
a CELL role.

The durable gain is graded by the remaining continuous participation
magnitude using the already-established local return strength. There is no
Boolean eligibility predicate, deadline, expiry timestamp, CELL kind, target
resistance, or task-selected lifetime.

## Frozen gates

### 1 -- supported participating CELL

An ordinary CELL fires, retains local participation, and then receives
qualified Modulation locally. Its resistance increases and its subsequent
ordinary local-decay lifetime extends accordingly.

### 2 -- use alone is not persistence

The same ordinary CELL fires repeatedly but receives no Modulation. Its
resistance remains unchanged and its durable lifetime remains the ordinary
CL0 lifetime for that resistance.

### 3 -- Modulation without participation

Modulation arrives locally, but the CELL did not fire and retains no local
participation. Resistance and durable decay state do not change.

### 4 -- wrong local compartment

One CELL participates; qualified Modulation arrives at a neighboring ordinary
CELL. The participating CELL does not consolidate.

### 5 -- relaxed participation

A CELL participates and is kept physically alive independently, but its
continuous participation relaxes fully before Modulation arrives. Late
Modulation produces no CELL-resistance change.

### 6 -- two nearby participating CELLs

Two ordinary nearby CELLs participate. Qualified Modulation arrives at only
one. Only that physical CELL consolidates.

### 7 -- repeated supported consequence

Repeated genuine participation followed by qualified local consequence
produces progressively greater resistance and proportionally longer ordinary
local-decay lifetime.

### 8 -- support stops

After consolidation, no further supported consequence occurs. Even the
consolidated CELL eventually reaches zero resistance and dies through ordinary
CL0 local decay.

### 9 -- retained CL0 lifetime/reuse

CL0 Gates 1--8 remain exact: proportional and phase-invariant CELL lifetime,
resident-slot reuse, generation advancement, stale incoming/outgoing
references, and independent orphan topology decay.

### 10 -- representation and replay

For every CC0 world, phase, and fresh-identity permutation, Reference and
Production mechanics must agree exactly on:

- ordered physical transitions;
- Drive and Modulatory deliveries;
- CELL resistance and death events;
- CELL participation, resistance, durable decay state, generation, and live
  state;
- final durable body, pending activity, clock, and PhysicalWork;
- natural quiescence; and
- exact same-mechanics replay.

ExecutionCost and resident capacity bookkeeping remain non-physical.
