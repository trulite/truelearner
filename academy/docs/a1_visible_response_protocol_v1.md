# A1 Visible Learned Response — Development Protocol v1

Status: frozen before behavioral execution.

## Purpose

Replace the schematic A1 cross with an honest, legible experience while
leaving the accepted TrueLearner physics and A1 learning geometry unchanged.

The Playground presents a generated creature and chime as the two visible
cues. Supported teaching uses the existing two completed physical loops. A
fresh probe presents the same cue relation without teaching or modulation.

## Output rule

The organism canvas may show the creature waving only when the recorded A1
experience contains at least one actual outward relation crossing. Zero
outward relation crossings must render a quiet/listening state.

The creature name, colors, captions, and artwork are Academy/world boundary
presentation. They never enter TrueLearner as an expected answer, capability
identifier, reward, pass/fail value, or semantic label.

## Controls

- supported teaching, then fresh learned-relation probe: one crossing and a
  visible wave;
- adjacent unsupported repetitions, then the same probe: zero crossings and
  no wave;
- echo, distractor, wrong-context, and unsupported-return controls: zero
  relation crossings and no learned response;
- probe: zero plasticity updates;
- selected replay: exact crossings, body, clock, work, and quiescence;
- stored episode: world and organism rasters remain content-addressed evidence.

## Stop condition

This is an Academy presentation successor only. Do not alter TrueLearner laws,
advance A2, or begin R7. After targeted headless regression and one native
visual verification, stop with the Playground running for human review.
