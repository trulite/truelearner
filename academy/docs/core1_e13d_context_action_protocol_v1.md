## Question

For each of the four frozen contexts, where does the context-to-action chain
first break?

1. context produces ordinary physical activity;
2. variation produces candidate action topology;
3. candidate topology traverses;
4. activity crosses the motor/output boundary;
5. the next supplied world frame differs;
6. consequence is admitted and Modulation returns;
7. candidate participation remains at consequence;
8. candidate coupling/resistance changes;
9. a fresh context presentation re-executes the route.

## Classification

Classify each profile at its first broken link:

- A: no candidate action topology appears;
- B: candidate topology appears but does not produce motor/output crossing;
- C: an action occurs but no world consequence returns;
- D: consequence returns but candidate material does not change;
- E: candidate material changes but fresh context does not re-execute;
- POSITIVE: the complete chain closes.

variation. No repair, ARC execution, physics interpretation, or authority
