## Diagnostic chain

Classify the first broken link per profile:

1. candidate exists;
2. candidate traverses;
3. participation remains at consequence;
4. qualified Modulation reaches the correct contact;
5. coupling changes;
6. inhibitor re-executes in probe;
7. coupling crosses the RB0 stability boundary;
8. probe settles.

The allowed final classes are:

- A: topology never exists/is selected;
- B: participation is gone before consequence;
- C: consequence arrives but efficacy does not change;
- D: efficacy matures but does not reach the boundary;
- E: boundary is reached but the inhibitor does not execute/stabilize;
- POSITIVE: every link is observed.
