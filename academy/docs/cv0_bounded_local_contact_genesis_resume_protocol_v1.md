# CV0 bounded local contact genesis resume protocol v1

Status: frozen after CC0 positive and before any CV0 candidate change.

Parent: CC0 positive `6ea3e82`.

This resumes, but does not amend, the frozen CV0 protocol
`cv0-bounded-local-contact-genesis-protocol-v1`. Its sole candidate proposal
form, gate order, controls, prohibitions, and stop rule remain binding.

## Resolved prerequisite

The former Gate-D stop is now independently resolved:

- CL0 gives every ordinary CELL phase-free finite lifetime, generation-safe
  death, and resident-slot reuse;
- CC0 lets actual ordinary CELL participation plus local qualified consequence
  consolidate CELL resistance; and
- use alone still cannot consolidate a CELL.

CV0 may use those cumulative ordinary laws. It may not add contact-specific
lifetime, cleanup, consolidation, or semantics.

## Candidate boundary

One eligible local source/target opportunity creates exactly two ordinary
candidate relations:

```text
P -> C+ -> X   outgoing candidate coupling +1
P -> C- -> X   outgoing candidate coupling -1
```

Each `C` is an ordinary threshold-1, resistance-1 CELL. Each of the four
ARROWs is an ordinary resistance-1 Drive ARROW. The two contacts have the same
physical position, region, threshold, resistance, delay, phase, generation
discipline, and proposal instant; only the outgoing candidate sign differs.

The relation is considered durably mature only if its ordinary contact and
the ordinary connections required to execute `P -> C -> X` remain executable
beyond their unsupported weak lifetime. This does not add a new gate; it makes
the original protocol's candidate-relation language mechanically observable.

Candidate creation may inspect only present local physical topology to avoid
duplicating an already-resident opportunity. It may not mark a CELL as a
contact or remember a selected candidate.

## Execution order

Run A through D first. Only if all pass, continue to E, then F through J in the
original order. Stop on the first failure and do not repair the candidate
inside CV0.

SV1, RS2, CE1, FD2, ARC, authority, the oracle, and `arch.md` remain unchanged
until CV0 itself is frozen positive.
