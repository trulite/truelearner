## Status

adoption or any post-adoption E14 observation. This is adoption of the single

## Earned primitive

Promote the following rule into candidate CORE1 physics:

> A causal route participation event may create its own temporary physical
> path for later consequence at the moment that participation occurs.

For the Academy action body, ordinary generated-contact Drive emission creates
the same E22 temporary Modulatory edge from the body's fixed returning CELL to
that contact. The edge endpoints remain its entire state. It carries no action,
route, path, episode, context, outcome, reward, timer, deadline, or passive
pending payload.

## Sole adoption seam

The E22 core mechanism and predicates remain byte-identical. Candidate
adoption changes only the ordinary CORE1 Academy integration:

1. body construction registers the existing returning CELL as the atomic
   consequence source and enables the candidate by default;
2. every ordinary action admission enables atomic capture only for the
   duration of that physical admission;
3. an admitted ordinary consequence traverses the live temporary topology and
   then removes it;
4. no consequence leaves the topology live, without timeout or silence
   cleanup.

The existing opt-in E22 methods may remain as diagnostics/compatibility
surfaces, but they may not implement different physics after adoption.

No continuation, OPEN marker, completion receptor, refractory rule, variation
cycle, chooser, babble change, PQLC change, gain change, pressure change,
sensor change, action map, or new evaluator accommodation is adopted here.
E14's frozen babbling curriculum already supplies the participating route.

## Execution discipline

Before evidence, require formatting, `git diff --check`, strict release Clippy
for the unchanged E14 evaluator, an unchanged evaluator hash, and a static
adoption-surface audit. These checks may compile but may not execute E14.

Then emit the evaluator's existing one-shot evidence marker and run the
unchanged E14 evaluator exactly once into a new result directory. Do not
overwrite the frozen negative.

Record complete Reference/replay/Production rows, hashes, and whether the
unchanged acceptance predicate passes. A negative is frozen without repair.
A positive closes the E14 frontier for this candidate but does not silently
grant broader ARC A3-A5 or architectural authority.

## Rejection screen

Reject the adoption result if:

- the E14 evaluator or fixture changes;
- return topology is reconstructed after admission;
- capture remains enabled outside physical action admission;
- passive USED-PENDING protection is enabled;
- consequence directly changes material instead of traversing topology into
  unchanged PQLC;
- consequence fails to remove temporary topology;
- a timeout, silence detector, episode/action identity, or route list appears;
- Reference replay, Reference/Production equality, bounded work, or natural
  quiescence fails.
