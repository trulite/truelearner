# CR0 coupling-necessity discriminator protocol v1

Status: frozen before evaluator implementation or execution.

Parent: `fd1-consequence-consolidation-ready-v3` (`59e85d5`). FD2 v1
remains an immutable stopped negative on its separate branch and is not a CR0
parent.

## Question

Does consequence-dependent coupling plasticity have an independently necessary
physical function, distinct from consequence-dependent resistance plasticity?

The quantities under comparison have separate physical meanings:

```text
coupling   = impulse exerted when a live ARROW traverses
resistance = durable persistence against local forgetting
```

CR0 is not allowed to make CPC0 pass, select a preferred lineage, or change a
physical law. It compares two post-consequence durable states already present
in frozen evidence:

```text
A  persistence only       coupling 1, resistance 4
B  efficacy + persistence coupling 2, resistance 4
```

The A state is the FD1 continuous-participation result. The B state is the
qualified LR-C result retained in the PX6 authority lineage. CR0 reconstructs
those equal-resistance durable states as starting conditions and observes their
future physical behavior under the single unchanged FD1 core. It does not add
a runtime learning switch or claim that the current core can create B.

## Matrix

Run two fresh identity roots, construction ages `0..9`, both
`MechanicalConfig::REFERENCE` and `MechanicalConfig::PRODUCTION`, and exact
same-mechanics replay.

Every continuation family runs with equal resistance and two coupling arms:

```text
P = coupling 1, resistance 4
E = coupling 2, resistance 4
```

### Retained-behavior probes

1. `cpc0_contact_locality`
   - two contact compartments participate;
   - Modulation reaches only contact A;
   - only A gains resistance in both arms;
   - coupling remains the arm's initial value.
2. `cpc1_temporal_participation`
   - prompt, delayed, unrelated-path, and same-path-renewed subcases;
   - plastic response follows remaining local participation in both arms.
3. `pqlc0_one_hop`
   - one local participation-qualified Modulatory relay traverses;
   - the source CELL does not fire because of Modulation.
4. `pqlc1_depth_16`
   - the unchanged local closure law composes through sixteen contacts;
   - exactly fifteen QLP relay traversals and natural quiescence occur.
5. `fd0_equal_persistence`
   - equal resistance and equal age yield the same death age despite different
     coupling.
6. `fd1_consolidation`
   - qualified Modulation increases resistance under both starting couplings;
   - traversal without Modulation does not;
   - the coupling value itself remains unchanged under the current FD1 law.

These probes assert scientific behavior, not `coupling == 2`. Reference and
Production must match exactly within each arm. The P and E physical traces are
not required to be byte-identical because their transmitted impulses are
physically different.

### Efficacy discriminator

Use a same-region candidate feeding a CELL whose firing drives a distinct
outward crossing. The candidate's own traversal is therefore not counted as
the output.

7. `threshold_1_already_executable`
   - P and E both fire the target and produce exactly one outward crossing.
8. `threshold_2_acquired_efficacy`
   - the pre-support baseline `coupling 1, resistance 1` is insufficient;
   - P remains insufficient despite equal consolidated resistance 4;
   - E fires the target and produces exactly one outward crossing.
9. `threshold_3_still_insufficient`
   - neither P nor E fires the target; one coupling increment is not a semantic
     success token.
10. `threshold_2_two_physical_inputs`
    - two distinct coupling-1 inputs may jointly fire the target;
    - this records that ordinary topology can supply efficacy while preserving
      the narrower fact that one learned route cannot.

For families 7--10 serialize target firings, outward crossings, delivered
impulses, candidate liveness/resistance/coupling, physical work, final clock,
quiescence, replay, and complete ordered physical-transition hashes.

## Representation gate

Within every arm and case, Reference and Production must match on:

- complete ordered physical transitions;
- Drive and Modulatory deliveries;
- fires, QLP traversals, resistance changes, proposals, deallocations, and
  crossings;
- PhysicalWork;
- final physical clock;
- canonical durable body;
- pending activity, natural quiescence, and exact replay.

ExecutionCost and resident capacity are mechanical observations and are not
physical-equivalence fields.

## Static gate

The CR0 diff must contain zero changes to the physical core and zero new:

- coupling update or mutation after ARROW construction;
- runtime strategy/feature choosing P versus E;
- reward, correctness, route, path, predecessor, depth, or target metadata;
- ARC-specific timing, threshold, or curriculum surface;
- authority or oracle claim.

The evaluator may choose initial `ArrowSpec.coupling` because the two frozen
durable states being compared differ in that physical field.

## Decision

`coupling necessary` requires all retained-behavior probes to remain lawful and
the threshold-2 discriminator to show an output available only to E, while the
threshold-1 and threshold-3 controls pass. This establishes an independent
physical role: supported history can make a previously subthreshold single
route effective, not merely persistent.

`coupling unnecessary` requires all retained probes to pass and no meaningful
behavioral difference in the efficacy discriminator.

Any representation mismatch, retained-behavior loss, instability, or mixed
benefit/harm is `unresolved`; stop for a separate investigation.

CR0 cannot edit CPC0's historical contract or resume FD2. After CR0 is frozen,
a separately preregistered integration or supersession gate must make that
lineage decision.
