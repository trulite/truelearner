## Frozen mechanism

The experimental substrate is an ordinary local CELL/ARROW/SPIKE network. One
event CELL emits into two independently executable learned routes. Repeated
route-local coactivation installs supporter CELLs and executable ARROW paths;
the support count is structural, not a probability. Each route terminates in a
contender CELL and a distinct effect CELL. The contender CELL that reaches its
fixed firing threshold first emits its effect spike and a local inhibitory
spike to the other contender. There is no action menu, route probability,
categorical sample, softmax, temperature, route scorer, evaluator-selected
winner, or random handle selection.

Supporter paths are local and have integer propagation delays determined by
their geometry. Later consolidated supporters occupy successively shorter
local paths, so additional learned support can change threshold-crossing time
through ordinary spike arrival. Every realized route must be independently
executable when the competing route is absent. Evaluator code may normalize
the post-run effect and count frequencies but cannot alter queue contents,
activation, inhibition, firing, or route validity.

The complete physical state includes all CELL/ARROW fields, pending SPIKEs,
transient recent activation, local competing activity, propagation timing,
arrival-order phase, generations, and liveness. Its canonical byte encoding
and fingerprint are recorded before each run.

## Ordered checks

### SSA0-A: exact physical replay

Run an equal-support event twice from canonical byte-identical complete state.
Pass only if starting bytes/fingerprints, full spike trace, realized effect,
ending fingerprint, and work ledger are duplicate-exact. Determinism is an
allowed result.

### SSA0-B: microscopic transient history

Hold permanent CELL/ARROW structure byte-identical and execute the balanced
history matrix. Pass only if both distinct executable routes are realized at
least once, every run realizes at most one route, and repeating each physical
history is duplicate-exact. This is sufficient transient sensitivity; if it
passes, SSA0-C is skipped and explicit stochastic physics is classified as not
required by the tested substrate.

### SSA0-C: conditional minimal physical noise

Not run when SSA0-B passes. If SSA0-B fails, stop after freezing that negative
and an explicit noise amendment; do not silently add or tune noise in the same
implementation/result.

### SSA0.1: support reshapes empirical realization frequency

For the seven fixed support pairs, measure route frequencies externally over
the 45 physical histories. Pass the narrow signature only if:

- equal support realizes each route in 30% through 70% of histories;
- each slight-bias favored route realizes more than 50% but less than 95%;
- each moderate favored frequency is at least its corresponding slight favored
  frequency and at least 65%;
- each large-bias favored route realizes at least 95%;
- mirrored A/B cases differ by no more than one of 45 trials after physical
  route permutation.

No frequency is supplied to or stored by the organism. A passing claim is
limited to learned/local structural support changing the empirical
distribution of physically realized affordances without an explicit
probability representation.

### SSA0.2: genuinely spatiotemporal competition

Independently perturb path length, spatial propagation distance, source-spike
timing, equal-tick arrival order, supporting-spike count, recent local
activation, competing local activity, and stored structural support. For each
factor, mirrored physical perturbations must produce the mirrored winner in at
least one near-tie pair while all other fields are fixed.

The required crossed cases are fixed as:

1. A stored support `8`, B support `6`, but B is three ticks earlier: B wins;
2. B stored support `8`, A support `6`, but A is three ticks earlier: A wins;
3. equal `6:6` support with A one path tick longer: B wins, and mirror: A wins;
4. equal timing with stored support `7:6`: A wins, and mirror: B wins.

The static audit rejects a production path containing an action-selection RNG,
probability representation, semantic route/effect inspection before
realization, aggregate route scoring, or an introduced `noise + argmax`
chooser. Queue ordering may inspect only physical arrival tick, local phase,
spike origin position, and stable physical identity.

## Controls

1. removing the second executable route yields the sole route in every history
   and no artificial variation;
2. two opaque evaluator aliases for one physical root normalize to one
   affordance and do not create a second route;
3. two different routes mapped to the same observable effect still compete

5. route-label/handle permutation preserves the physical relation and mirrors
   evaluator labels only;

9. each route, alone, reaches its own nonempty effect through live ARROWs;
