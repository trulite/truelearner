# DS4--DS8 de-supply target freeze

Protocol identifier: `ds4-ds8-target-freeze-v1`

Status: **FROZEN TARGETS; NO MECHANISM OR RESULT PREREGISTERED**.

This document freezes only the remaining supplied meanings and the information
that each de-supply stage forbids. It does not choose a repair, representation,
learning rule, cumulative matrix, or program priority. Each cumulative stage
must begin from its authoritative parent and preserve every earlier de-supply.

The serial scientific order is:

```text
M3 -> DS4 -> M4 -> DS5 -> M5 -> DS6 -> M6 -> DS7 -> M7 -> DS8 -> M8
```

Later targets may be studied in isolated development before their parent
exists, but isolated work cannot advance this lineage.

## Common boundary

Allowed at every stage:

- ordinary cells, arrows, spikes, propagation, and local activity;
- fresh occurrence-local handles and opaque payload identity where already
  present in the authoritative parent;
- learned correspondence, roles, causal direction, and event organization
  already earned by the cumulative lineage;
- physical downstream effects and evaluator-blind runtime accounting;
- evaluator-only fixtures, expected traces, and scoring that are type- and
  path-separated from organism acquisition, persistence, selection,
  execution, and update paths, except for a supply explicitly scheduled for a
  later de-supply stage.

Forbidden at every stage:

- restoring any meaning removed by an earlier stage under another name;
- stable evaluator object identity, truth-derived fingerprints, future route
  or destination, hidden correlation tokens, or answer-bearing metadata;
- using a definitive result to tune, rescue, or reinterpret its mechanism.

The sequential ladder deliberately leaves later supplies intact until their
own stage. Their continued presence is a named limitation, not permission to
use them to reconstruct an earlier forbidden meaning.

## DS4 -- request/start

Removed supply:

> A distinguished request/query and an externally supplied instruction that
> says when its computation starts.

Forbidden organism-visible information:

- request, query, target, or start labels, flags, opcodes, populations, ports,
  or dedicated host calls;
- a target identity passed separately from the ordinary anonymous occurrence
  activity in which it appears;
- serialization position, receptor identity, parser field, queue token, or
  stable surface marker that denotes the request;
- evaluator answer identity or correctness entering request selection or
  initiation.

Lawful physical evidence includes anonymous identity-carrying occurrences,
observable local structure, learned M3 event activity, ordinary recurrence,
and the later terminal signal that remains explicitly supplied until DS8.
Success requires both selecting the request-bearing occurrence and initiating
the frozen downstream computation without a supplied `START` meaning.

## DS5 -- finish/output

Removed supply:

> A distinguished finish condition and an externally supplied instruction to
> emit the current value as output/answer.

Forbidden organism-visible information:

- finish, done, stop, output, answer, or emit labels, flags, opcodes,
  populations, ports, or dedicated host branches;
- an evaluator-provided answer route or answer identity;
- a host interpretation of no-result, queue state, event closure, or current
  value as semantically final;
- expected length, remaining steps, or task depth.

Lawful physical evidence includes learned M4 initiation, structural absence or
no-successor activity, event closure, queue quiescence, current local state,
ordinary consequences, and the terminal signal still supplied until DS8.
Success requires the organism's learned path to produce the externally
observable value and quiesce without supplied finish/output meaning.

## DS6 -- lifetime/persistence

Removed supply:

> Preclassified temporary/permanent state and supplied lifetime or persistence
> classes.

Forbidden organism-visible information:

- temporary, permanent, episode, session, retain, erase, lifetime, persistence,
  cache, or memory-tier labels or enums;
- evaluator task boundaries, reuse horizon, future use count, break-even,
  retention oracle, or privileged cleanup instruction;
- storage class chosen from semantic state kind rather than earned physical
  history and local use.

Lawful physical evidence includes ordinary recurrence, use, disuse,
replacement pressure, local storage/work cost, contradiction, dependency
history, event activity, and learned M5 closure. Success requires state to
remain or disappear through one evaluator-blind physical lifecycle while
preserving the frozen downstream behavior.

## DS7 -- plasticity targeting

Removed supply:

> A privileged rule that says where plasticity may occur or which proposal
> should be formed and tested.

Forbidden organism-visible information:

- target, proposal, candidate, eligible-location, relevant-population, or
  correct-edge lists supplied by the evaluator or task author;
- task-, operation-, phase-, answer-, or route-specific growth slots;
- a hand-written branch that opens the edge needed by the frozen downstream
  mechanism;
- global search ranked by evaluator success, expected topology, or future
  utility.

Lawful physical evidence includes co-activity, local adjacency, propagation,
eligibility traces, differential consequences, competition, ordinary
resource pressure, and learned M6 lifecycles. Success requires proposals and
their sites to arise from those local physical conditions and still support
the frozen cumulative behavior.

## DS8 -- semantic credit

Removed supply:

> Privileged semantic `correct`/`wrong`, reward, or answer-comparison credit.

Forbidden organism-visible information:

- correct, wrong, success, failure, reward, loss, score, label, expected answer,
  or evaluator-truth values reaching acquisition, eligibility, selection,
  persistence, execution, or update paths;
- any sign or scalar computed from equality with a target answer or expected
  trace;
- a terminal teaching event whose polarity is assigned by task semantics;
- semantic credit hidden as route choice, timing, magnitude, omission, reset,
  or namespace.

Lawful evidence is limited to evaluator-blind physical downstream consequence,
returned activity, contrast, differential support, local resource effects,
and the learned M7 proposal/lifecycle machinery. Evaluator truth may score
held-out behavior only after execution and may never feed back into the
organism. Success requires the cumulative behavior to be acquired and retained
without privileged semantic credit.

## Sprint finish condition

If and only if cumulative DS8 passes its single frozen definitive matrix, M8
becomes authoritative. The de-supply program then closes rather than inventing
additional semantic removals. A final irreducible-substrate manifest must
freeze what remains supplied, why it is treated as physical substrate, and the
complete M0--M8 lineage. Scaling, generativity, language, stochastic
affordances, tool use, longer-horizon learning, and compute/memory scaling then
become capability questions rather than blockers on this ladder.
