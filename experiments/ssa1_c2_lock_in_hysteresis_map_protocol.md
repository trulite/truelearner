## Research question

> Does Frozen Organism v1 retain a finite ordinary-experience barrier after
> maturation, or do its learned dynamics create an absorbing state for the
> tested class of counterexperience?

The map must locate the first edge at which new physical evidence ceases to
change future learned structure:

```text
B physically executes
  -> M6 observes B consequence
  -> M6 emits contrary differential or abstains
  -> M5 receives or does not receive an update
  -> M5 value changes or does not change
  -> plastic admission changes or does not change
  -> live supporter count changes or does not change
```

## Map axes

### H — initial A maturation

From blank starts, give route A exactly:

```text
0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 24, 32, 64, 192
```

stable ordinary physical executions before changing the world to A-varying /
B-stable. Record the full frozen audit at the change boundary.

### E — later B-world evidence

At every H checkpoint, run two separately reported schedules:

1. **E0 — B-only counterexperience.** The mature C1 construction physically
   enables B, returns B's stable consequence, and leaves A unavailable during
   that interval. This asks whether more B evidence alone can reverse the
   frozen history.
2. **E1 — paired changed-world experience.** Each evidence unit contains one
   physically enabled B-stable execution followed by one ordinary A-varying
   opportunity. If A remains executable, its consequence variants alternate
   `1, 3`; if it does not execute, it receives no evidence. This asks whether
   the whole changed environment can both establish B and erode A's earlier
   consequence regularity.

For both schedules, measure after cumulative B-opportunity budgets:

```text
0, 4, 16, 64, 1,024, 10,000
```

At H=`16, 32, 192`, extend one frozen trajectory to `100,000` B
opportunities.
Budgets are cumulative within a trajectory so the experiment does not replay
shorter prefixes merely for accounting.

One consequence may be returned only after a genuine threshold-closing route
execution. No unexecuted route receives evidence. E0 and E1 must never be
pooled: a finite barrier in E1 does not erase an absorbing E0 result, and an E0
absorber does not justify claiming global irreversibility if E1 reverses.

### S — physical support available to B

At mature H=`192`, sweep additional ordinary pre-closure background support:

```text
0, 1, 2, 3 early spikes
```

The background is defined by local physical layout and mirrored with layout;
it contains no route semantics. The same pulses after closure are an inert
control. This axis separates:

- evidence absent because B cannot execute;
- B executes but M6 does not generate a contrary update;
- M6 updates but M5 does not move;
- M5 moves but supporter formation does not follow.

### T — disuse / ordinary pressure

At mature H=`192`, insert exactly:

```text
0, 16, 64, 256, 1,024
```

pressure events with neither A nor B encountered. Then restore the same B
world with three lawful early background spikes and run 10,000 episodes.

This tests whether ordinary forgetting reopens physical executability, erases
the credit barrier, erases both, or merely destroys the learned landscape. A
blanked landscape is reported as forgetting, not successful reversal.

## Chain audit

For every H/E/S/T checkpoint record:

- schedule (`E0` or `E1`), actual A/B realizations, and unresolved episodes;
- consequences returned and M6 observations added;
- M6 shape count, leading support, leading margin, and eligibility per route;
- M6 abstentions added;
- M5 differential applications added;
- M5 support, rejection, score, and value resistance per route;
- live supporters, proposal resistance, and future admissions per route;
- exploration admissions and ordinary pressure exposure;
- whether B independently executes after environmental background is removed.

The first non-responsive edge is defined mechanically:

1. **execution edge:** B never crosses threshold;
2. **observation edge:** B crosses but M6 does not observe;
3. **credit edge:** M6 observes but emits no B-favoring differential;
4. **allocation edge:** B-favoring differential exists but M5 does not change;
5. **formation edge:** M5 changes but live support does not;
6. **none:** live support and independent execution reverse.

The evaluator may label this edge only after comparing the recorded physical
states. The label cannot enter the organism.

## Mechanistic invariant audit

Without changing code, statically and dynamically verify whether the tested
mature state has this invariant:

```text
A recurrent consequence eligible
+ B later becomes recurrent consequence eligible
  -> M6 sees [eligible, eligible]
  -> M6 abstains
  -> no new M5 differential

and M6 evidence has no ordinary pressure/decay path
  -> more same-class B evidence cannot break the tie
```

If every premise is established from frozen source and runtime counters, the
result may classify the state as absorbing for this preregistered ordinary
counterexperience class, rather than merely “not reversed by 100,000.” It may
not generalize to untested consequence structures or environments.

## Development classifications

- **A — finite reversal barrier:** at least one fully mature H checkpoint
  reverses through a finite amount of preregistered ordinary changed-world
  experience, and the reversal remains independently executable after
  background removal. E0 and E1 are reported separately.
- **B — moving but uncrossed barrier:** mature internal states move
  monotonically toward B through 100,000 observations without crossing, and no
  exact frozen invariant prevents eventual reversal.
- **C — absorbing credit state:** B executes and M6 observes it, but mature M6
  credit reaches a non-decaying abstention invariant; M5 receives no later
  contrary differential and the landscape remains locked regardless of tested
  evidence budget in both E0 and E1.
- **D — downstream allocation/formation absorber:** M6 emits B-favoring
  differentials but M5 allocation or supporter formation remains invariant.
- **E — forgetting-only reopening:** disuse restores physical alternatives
  only by erasing learned state rather than reversing it, and C or D otherwise
  holds.
- **F — scientific ambiguity:** the frozen substrate cannot separate the
  candidate barriers with evaluator-only observation.

Report forgetting-only behavior orthogonally even when the strongest primary
classification is C or D.
