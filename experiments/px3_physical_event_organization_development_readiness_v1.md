## Result

PX3's development chain now establishes, with unchanged PX0 physics:

```text
distinct physical participation traces overlap
  -> a joint opportunity physically fires
  -> native local proposal forms a weak organization
  -> the organization itself participates
  -> downstream participation plus ordinary return creates a unit echo
  -> eligible organization strengthens without its source refiring
  -> recurrence earns persistence
  -> absent support plus ordinary pressure removes obsolete organization
  -> changed experience forms a fresh organization
  -> a mature organization emits one ordinary unit participation trace
  -> the identical process recurses at the next level
```

provenance identifier, composite flag, level flag, semantic return channel or
special deletion operation.
