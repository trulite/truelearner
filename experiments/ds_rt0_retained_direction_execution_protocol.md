- CP0 produces exactly one consolidated template from equal initial probation;
- after freezing its support map, no further `observe` call occurs;
- the same learner installs exactly one route on fresh disjoint occurrences;

- fresh occurrence IDs, relabeling, handle permutation, and allocation/layout
  perturbations preserve transfer;
- ambiguous, unstable, suppressed, or removed D3 direction installs no route;
- no concrete occurrence, opaque handle, episode identity, direction slot,
  correctness, reward, polarity, or causal label is persistent;
- RT0 introduces no learner, candidate, installer, or executor type.
