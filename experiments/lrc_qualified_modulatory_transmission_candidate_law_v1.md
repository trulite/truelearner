# LR-C qualified modulatory transmission candidate law v1

Status: **FROZEN SUCCESSOR-LAW CANDIDATE; NOT AUTHORITATIVE**.

## Forced boundary

Authoritative PX0 established that recently traversed local structure can be
strengthened by later physical activity. Continuous PX3 development exposed
that an undifferentiated arrival at the eligible source aliases renewed
upstream execution with downstream learning evidence.

Fresh development eliminated cheaper proposed resolutions:

- timing windows overlap;
- route-aware return can close an excitatory oscillator;
- a separate ordinary learning compartment is contaminated by dense ordinary
  topology; and
- a distinct physical transmission effect passed the registered dense and
  cumulative development controls.

The candidate is therefore a successor to the generic PX0 return law, not a
reinterpretation of spent PX0--PX2 authority.

## Minimal law

> An ARROW traversal has one of two physical transmission effects: **Drive** or
> **Modulatory**. Drive may change the target CELL's activation and cause
> ordinary execution but cannot directly change eligible structure.
> Modulatory transmission may change recently eligible outgoing ARROW
> structure at its target CELL but does not change that CELL's activation and
> cannot itself cause the CELL to fire.

The distinction is physical and non-semantic. Neither mode carries valence,
reward, correctness, outcome identity, contributor identity, route ownership
or causal labels.

## Exact local mechanics

The candidate implementation is the frozen LR1-C source:

`crates/lr1-modulatory-physical-return/src/lib.rs`

SHA-256:
`7226a0e4af0ff484c6fd61c46c9073ce8363692100c2a090b0ce64483f3cfc10`.

Its complete active-state addition is one two-valued `TransmissionMode` field
on every ARROW:

```text
Drive | Modulatory
```

Delivery is exhaustive:

```text
Drive traversal arrives at CELL P
→ ordinary state decay/update and threshold check
→ P may fire
→ no eligible outgoing ARROW is strengthened by this arrival

Modulatory traversal arrives at CELL P
→ inspect live outgoing ARROWs sourced at P
→ each ARROW whose existing eligibility window is live receives
  the existing local return update
→ consumed eligibility closes
→ P activation is unchanged
→ P threshold is not checked
→ P does not fire
```

External arrivals are ordinary Drive. Modulatory effect can occur only through
actual traversal of a live Modulatory ARROW. Eligibility is still created only
by actual execution/traversal of the candidate source and outgoing structure.
Expired eligibility, absent eligibility and dead/generation-stale structure
cannot be updated.

The retained PX0 magnitudes remain unchanged:

- local eligibility window: four ticks;
- accepted return: resistance `+3`;
- positive coupling maturation: at most `+1`, capped at coupling `2`;
- eligibility is consumed by an accepted update;
- ordinary pressure, proposal, deallocation and all Drive execution physics
  remain unchanged.

## Candidate claim

The law candidate claims only physical separation between transmission that
executes and transmission that modulates eligible plasticity. It does not
claim that every Modulatory path is useful or correct. Experience and topology
still determine which modulatory paths physically occur; a misleading but
genuinely traversed Modulatory path may lawfully change eligible local
structure.

Authority must therefore test mode effect, eligibility coincidence, dense
ordinary non-leakage, mode swapping, multiple lawful local updates, fresh
identity/layout invariance, quiescence, exact replay and fresh cumulative
PX0--PX2 successor conformance. PX3 is outside this law authority workflow.
