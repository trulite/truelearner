## Active-path state restriction

Organism-visible persistent and transient state may consist only of:

- CELL identity, position, region, threshold, local mutable state, generation,
  resistance, and liveness;
- ARROW endpoints, coupling, delay/phase, generation, resistance, and liveness;
- SPIKE origin, destination, impulse, tick/phase/order, and ordinary queue
  presence;
- generic local eligibility or refractory state only if physically attached
  to the above and updated without semantic branching;
- physical boundary crossings and returned physical activity.

The active path must not contain `RelationMotif`, `RelationAtom`,
`AnonymousView`, `CandidateLink`, `Episode`, `History`, `Neighborhood`,
`Query`, truth/filler identifiers, correctness booleans, correspondence keys,
pattern hashes, semantic labels, or renamed equivalents.

A generic Rust container is not automatically lawful. If its fields jointly
encode which arrivals constitute a motif, candidate relation, episode, or
answer, it is a forbidden representation regardless of its name.

## Physical input boundary

The evaluator/world may create fresh cells, layouts, and anonymous spike
schedules. It may impose ordinary downstream physical consequences. It may
measure emitted/crossed activity. It may not tell the active mechanism:

- which arrivals belong together;
- which candidate is the correspondence;
- when an episode begins or ends;
- whether an attempt is correct or successful;
- which local structure to strengthen, weaken, create, or remove.

The active mechanism may not call `begin_episode()`, clear transient state at
an evaluator boundary, inspect an execution trace and then apply host-side
learning, or receive evaluator-selected mutation targets.

## Lawful physicalization work

PX0 may introduce a generic local plastic execution law only where the
retained substrate contract already permits local structural variation and
strengthening/weakening. Such a law must:

1. apply uniformly to anonymous local physical configurations;
2. derive eligibility only from ordinary local activity and timing;
3. receive consequence only as ordinary physical return activity;
4. store its result only in CELL/ARROW state or topology;
5. have no branch on evaluator semantics or route identity;
6. transfer under fresh identities and layout/allocation permutations.

If more than one scientifically distinct local rule is equally plausible and
the physical evidence cannot distinguish them, PX0 stops as scientific
ambiguity rather than selecting one for convenience.

## Behavioral oracle

The old authoritative M0 implementation is a reference implementation, not a
dependency. PX0 is compared at the externally observable behavioral level:

1. recurring relational physical experience produces one usable
   correspondence;
2. the learned correspondence supports the same downstream physical effect;
3. missing correspondence produces no effect;
4. two equally supported incompatible correspondences produce no arbitrary
   effect;
5. a changed physical neighborhood invalidates the old executable path;
6. return of the historical physical neighborhood permits lawful reuse when
   its structure remains live;
7. fresh occurrence/cell identities preserve behavior;
8. allocation, handle, memory, and layout permutations preserve the physical
   relation;
9. exact complete-state replay remains exact;
10. activity becomes naturally quiescent without an episode reset;
11. work and persistent storage are explicitly accounted.

Observable equivalence does not require the old internal fingerprint or old
CELL/ARROW layout. It forbids weaker success criteria invented after seeing a
result.

## Development stages

### PROBE

Use one tiny blank substrate and one fresh namespace. Establish only whether
recurring anonymous local activity plus ordinary physical consequence can
leave a persistent executable correspondence path. Include immediate missing,
ambiguous, duplicate-exact, and no-reset controls.

PROBE stops immediately if the proposed learner requires an intermediate
pattern object, evaluator-selected plasticity, trace-to-mutation host logic,
or semantic boundary.

### MICRO

If PROBE is positive, use approximately two fresh namespaces and the unchanged
physical law. Test acquisition, held-out execution, missing/ambiguous paths,
physical-neighborhood change and return, fresh identities, and basic layout
permutation.

### GATE

If MICRO is positive, harden with fresh namespaces/layouts, multiple lawful
timing/topology presentations, all behavioral-oracle controls, static source
and dependency audits, exact replay, quiescence, work/storage accounting, and
frozen-parent hash verification.

No definitive execution is authorized. Development readiness must hand off to
a separate authority workflow.

## Classifications

- **PX0-A — substrate-native correspondence positive:** the unchanged local
  physical law learns and executes correspondence with the conjunctive
  behavioral controls and no semantic intermediate representation.
- **PX0-B — behavioral mismatch:** a lawful physical candidate exists but
  fails the frozen behavioral oracle.
- **PX0-C — substrate insufficiency:** the retained physical degrees of freedom
  cannot express acquisition without evaluator mutation or a forbidden
  intermediate representation.
- **PX0-D — scientific ambiguity:** multiple distinct physical laws or
  representations remain equally plausible and current evidence cannot select
  among them.

Every meaningful negative remains immutable. No negative may be rescued by
renaming a semantic struct, moving an adapter, weakening a control, or changing
the reference behavior.
