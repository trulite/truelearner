# FFS-SAME0 preregistration: learned filler correspondence

Protocol identifier: `ffs-same0-learned-correspondence-v1`

Status: frozen before FFS-SAME0 implementation, development measurement, or
any definitive FFS-SAME0 run.

## Question

FFS-SAME0 asks:

> Can filler continuity itself be acquired from anonymous temporal and causal
> structure as ordinary executable substrate structure, and can that learned
> correspondence support fresh binding, grounded execution, recursive
> compaction, and parent-relative economic retention without supplied filler
> equality?

The ablation is structural. It does not remove the integer identities required
for CELL/ARROW/SPIKE physics. It removes every organism-visible token,
comparison, payload, key, pointer, or provenance endpoint that preserves the
identity of an evaluator filler across observations.

FFS-SAME0 does not require cross-process closure. Learning, retrieval, and
decision remain `UNAVAILABLE` unless their actual computation already becomes
replaceable through the same anonymous substrate without a process-specific
adapter.

## Identity boundary

Two identity domains are distinct.

Allowed substrate identity:

```text
CellId
ArrowId
fresh invocation-local OccurrenceId
anonymous local event position
ordinary learned-structure identity
```

Forbidden supplied filler correspondence:

```text
stable filler or object ID across observations
SAME or equality result over fillers
pointer equality or canonical object representation
reused occurrence handle
correlation or workspace ID crossing observations
map key, queue token, hash, or fingerprint derived from evaluator truth
future object ID
shared provenance endpoint that survives from one observation to another
```

Evaluator truth uses a separate type confined to the harness. No conversion or
hash from that type may enter an organism-visible value. Every observation of
the same evaluator object receives a newly minted `OccurrenceId`. Occurrence
IDs are unique within the run and may not cross their invocation lifetime.

The evaluator may map an organism-selected current occurrence back to truth
only after execution, for scoring and observable comparison. That mapping may
not affect candidate formation, selection, credit except through the frozen
terminal consequence, consolidation, execution, or invalidation.

## Learner-visible interface

The organism receives only:

```text
fresh anonymous occurrence
local event position
temporal relation
causal-influence relation
local activity/coactivity
ordinary terminal success/failure
```

Provenance is relational, not identity-preserving. If an evaluator object is
observed as an output and later as an input, those observations use distinct
occurrence IDs. A causal relation may connect local event positions and fresh
occurrences as evidence, but it does not assert equality.

A direct causal edge is not sufficient evidence. Each candidate
correspondence is described by a local relational motif containing multiple
anonymous temporal/causal atoms. The matrix includes:

- the same superficial causal shape with different evaluator continuity;
- different causal shapes with the same evaluator continuity;
- direct-looking misleading provenance;
- interrupted continuity and historical return;
- locally ambiguous competing candidates.

Thus reading one causal edge as identity cannot pass the experiment.

## Learned correspondence lifecycle

During acquisition, ordinary local candidate correspondences are tried through
the current task. Only terminal success/failure supplies credit:

```text
successful candidate use  +2
failed candidate use      -1
consolidation threshold    6
prune threshold           -2
```

The learner receives no correct-pair label or trace equality. A persistent
correspondence rule may retain only an anonymous relational motif,
compatibility context, and ordinary strength. It may not retain an occurrence
ID or evaluator truth.

At use time, a consolidated correspondence rule selects a current fresh
occurrence from locally available candidates. The resulting temporary binding
maps an anonymous effect role to that current occurrence. Concrete observable
effects act through the current occurrence capability; the evaluator resolves
truth only after execution.

When evidence violates a learned continuity rule, the rule invalidates and
generic anonymous candidate processing reopens. When a historical context
returns, an unchanged compatible rule may reactivate without reacquisition.
There is no privileged identity-correction mechanism.

## Recursive execution

Once temporary bindings exist, the SAME-less execution uses one generic
CELL/ARROW/SPIKE substitution kernel. Primitive and learned arrows share one
persistent representation and one queue. A learned arrow firing emits an
ordinary occurrence that the same local observer may consume again.

For every promoted edge `k -> k+1`:

```text
functional:
    ObservableTrace(L(k+1)) == ObservableTrace(L(k))

computational:
    RuntimeWork(L(k+1)) < RuntimeWork(L(k))

economic:
    acquisition_k
  + installation_k
  + carrying_k(H)
  + H * (runtime(L(k+1)) + maintenance_k - runtime(L(k)))
    <= 0 for finite H
```

Every child earns existence against its immediate retained parent, never
against the primitive root after the first promotion. A profitable deeper
child cannot rescue a failed intermediate edge.

The organism does not see work, prices, horizons, level, workload depth,
identity population, evaluator truth, or economic retention decisions.

Two economic views are independent:

1. within the SAME-less organism, every recursive child is tested against its

2. the whole SAME-less mature stack, including identity acquisition and

FFS-SAME0 may be A/B/C positive while remaining more expensive than supplied

logical necessity.

Zero carrying price is primary. Secondary prices are `0, 1, 10, 100, 1000`
millionths of work per incremental persistent byte-use. Exact integer
millionths and ceiling division are authoritative. Horizons are analytical.

## Independent outcomes

### A — Correspondence reconstruction

Positive iff every definitive seed learns at least two valid relational
correspondence motifs without supplied filler equality, transfers them to fresh
occurrences and fresh evaluator identities, passes false-correspondence and all
leak audits, and invalidates stale incompatible correspondence.

### B — Functional recovery

Positive iff learned correspondence supports exact temporary binding,
role-relative transfer, grounded concrete execution, declared interruption
boundaries, changed bindings, and historical return for every definitive seed.

### C — Fractal recovery

Positive iff the same SAME-less kernel produces at least two consecutive exact,
physically cheaper, independently repayable parent-relative promotions at S1,
S2, and S3 for every definitive seed. Structural, justified, and realized
depth plus over/under-retention are reported exactly as in FFS0.

### D — Identity economics

Report independently:

- within-SAME-less marginal recursive economics;
- identity acquisition and carrying;
- whole mature SAME-less runtime versus frozen supplied-SAME FFS0;
- whole-stack break-even versus supplied SAME when finite.

D is `PARITY_OR_BETTER` only if the complete SAME-less stack reaches finite
break-even against supplied SAME at one or more preregistered primary anchors.
Otherwise it is `EXPENSIVE`, without weakening A/B/C.

### E — Process availability

Report execution, learning, retrieval, and decision independently as
`POSITIVE`, `NEGATIVE`, or `UNAVAILABLE`. A process becomes positive only when
its actual computation executes and is replaceable through the same anonymous
substrate. No adapter, semantic event class, or synthetic trace may be added.

## Stopping and interpretation

The first failed stage is recorded as the collapse point:

```text
correspondence -> binding -> grounding -> compaction -> recursive economics
```

A failure does not authorize a new primitive. An A/B/C positive but D-expensive
result establishes that supplied filler equality is unnecessary for the tested
execution capability but is a valuable computational prior. E may remain
unchanged.

The strongest eligible claim is:

> Identity correspondence was acquired from anonymous temporal and causal
> structure as ordinary substrate structure, and that learned correspondence
> supported fresh binding, executable programs, grounding, and recursive
> computational organization without supplied filler equality.

No claim extends beyond the frozen observable interface, workloads, prices,
process-availability boundary, or six-promotion censoring ceiling.
