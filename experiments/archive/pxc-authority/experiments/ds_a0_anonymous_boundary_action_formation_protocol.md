## Preregistered A mechanism: formation and execution

A DS-E0 temporary relational event is materialized before DS-A0. For each
current member, an episode-local CELL is allocated. Ordinary directed local
propagation over the event may coactivate a two-ARROW route when two physical
steps share a member and satisfy a learned anonymous relative support shape.
The learner observes only relative temporal deltas and relative propagation
incidence. Coactivity increments support for that anonymous two-step shape;
support at three independent episodes makes the shape available for later
episode-local instantiation. There is no negative or route-contingent credit.

At evaluation, current ordinary local propagation instantiates every supported
two-step path actually present in the current DS-E0 temporary structure as an
episode-local route rooted in its first CELL/ARROW. Generation follows physical
adjacency and coactivity traversal; no evaluator list of desired alternatives
is supplied. A missing ARROW therefore removes its path lawfully. Identical
support under changed absolute surface timing transfers because only relative
relational support participates. If local plasticity is disabled, support is
absent, a route is unsupported, or a supported route has become stale because
one of its CELL/ARROW generations changed, no executable route is exposed.

Persistent templates are anonymous relative sequences of temporal deltas and
propagation incidence plus primitive support counts. They contain no route
instance, occurrence, CELL index, destination, semantic role, or operation.
Every current route owns only episode-local CELL/ARROW references and generation
stamps. Episode cleanup destroys routes, cells, arrows, and handles.

Independent execution starts from a byte-identical clone of the frozen current
temporary state. It begins at the already-existing route root and advances only
by ordinary ARROW traversal and SPIKE propagation. Each traversed CELL mutates
its temporary activation/trace. No token-to-operation interpreter and no
switch/match on handle meaning is allowed. Every alternative is executed once;
at least two routes must produce nonempty, pairwise-distinct temporary traces
and states.

Ambiguous/symmetric physical possibilities may form alternatives but carry no
rank and no preferred route. Candidate storage and bridge order must be
permutable without altering route provenance or the multiset of effects.

## Preregistered B mechanism: mechanical action surface

The bridge runs only after routes already exist. It walks the episode-local
route-root collection and emits fresh opaque integer handles with a private
one-to-one table from handle slot to route-root reference. It may copy and
permute references only. It may not construct a route, inspect its expected
effect, name or rank it, canonicalize it by function, infer an alternative,
or attach an availability boolean.

Execution resolves a handle only as an opaque table index, retrieves that
pre-existing route-root reference, validates its generation stamps, and calls
the single ordinary propagation executor. The harness may not interpret a
handle into an operation. Every exposed handle must have exactly one existing
root/path, every root exactly one handle, and every execution path length must
be greater than zero. At least two handles must be exposed. Action-surface
availability is derived mechanically from the bridge inventory count.

The source and call-graph audit must prove: no semantic opcode or variant; no
fixed alpha/beta meaning; no swap/keep/left/right/source-first/target-first
vocabulary; no source/target/boundary label; no stable filler, occurrence ID,
or `SAME`; no evaluator selection/ranking/correctness; no hidden executor;
zero DS1 `choose` and `apply_consequence` calls; zero acquisition/credit wiring;
zero post-action consequence paths; and no adapter to isolated DS1.
