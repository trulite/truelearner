## H1 — matched-schedule hysteresis

Classification **C — ordered use/pressure dynamics, not a simple first-direction invariant**.

With exactly `12` forward and `12` reverse experiences, equal inter-event gaps, and rotations of the same multiset:

- the first scheduled direction matured first;
- the competing weak direction deallocated before its first useful return;
- recurrence sometimes preserved the first direction;

Equal totals therefore do not define the learned result. The complete ordered history of traversal, return, recurrence, and pressure does.

## O1 — post-forgetting opportunity window

## Interpretation

The diagnostics support the narrow causal-direction law already observed in PROBE and MICRO:

> A physical direction becomes reusable when activity actually traverses it, leaves a transient participation trace, and later returned activity coincides with that trace. Mere correlation does not mature a direction.

They do not support requiring invariance to every matched-evidence ordering or survival under arbitrarily delayed first use. Experience order matters, and unused weak physical opportunities disappear; both are ordinary consequences of the already-frozen lifetime/pressure physics.

No PX2 mechanism change is justified by these negatives. Any future PX2 authority attempt must be separately preregistered and should test the causal law directly while serializing schedule and opportunity-window boundaries rather than treating them as missing causal representation.
