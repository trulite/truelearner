## PX-C audit lane

PX-C is observational. It may inventory source seams, fixture lifecycle calls,
and typed handoffs, but it may not alter organism physics or reinterpret a lane
result.

The v1 active-surface manifest contains:

1. the authoritative LR-C successor substrate used by PX0--PX3; and
2. the exact predecessor implementation surfaces for PX4--PX8 that remain to
   be replaced on the continuous line.

The audit excludes frozen experiment prose, result files, evaluator-only arms,
binary shims, build scripts, and superseded PX0--PX3 mechanism sources. Those
artifacts remain part of the historical record, but counting their vocabulary
would not measure the continuous candidate.

The lexical inventory is deliberately conservative inside the manifested Rust
sources: comments and strings are counted along with executable occurrences.
This prevents convenient renaming or moving a fixture instruction into an
embedded evaluator label from making the count look smaller. Each matched
occurrence is serialized with category, layer, file, line, and matched text.

The categories are:

- typed `Episode`/`episode` concepts;
- typed or named history concepts;
- typed `Query`/`query` concepts;
- `begin_episode` calls or definitions;
- `erase_temporary` calls or definitions;
- seed-built event/history/session fixture construction;
- explicit calls into frozen mechanism namespaces;
- typed or frozen inter-layer handoffs.

The baseline is not expected to be zero. It records the remaining supply that
PX4--PX8 must remove from the cumulative candidate. On the serial authority
branch, the total may stay equal or decrease; it may not increase without a
new protocol version and an explicit scientific explanation. A manifest entry
may be replaced only when the corresponding successor layer becomes
authoritative.
