## Sole question

> Given two actual learned anonymous affordances and later ordinary world
> activity attributed to matched executions of each, can the substrate form a
> directional relation from a repeatable difference in downstream consequence
> structure, rather than from the identity or immediate effect of the executed
> route?

DS-D3 stops at that temporary directional relation. It must not call frozen
DS1, update boundary-role strengths, or test boundary-role recovery.

## Frozen inputs and temporal boundary

Use the exact frozen E0+A1 lineage and verify the frozen D2 and stage-11
collapse fingerprints. Every observation has three ordered parts:

1. one actual A1 route executes and produces its normal route-specific effect;
2. at least one local tick later, ordinary environment CELL/ARROW/SPIKE
   propagation produces a downstream anonymous consequence trace;
3. the existing episode-local attribution chain associates that later trace
   with the executable affordance that preceded it.

The downstream trace has its own fresh occurrence identities and must not copy
the immediate route trace, opaque handle, selected index, evaluator boundary
role, or target answer. Immediate effects are recorded only by the evaluator
to prove that both routes executed cleanly; they are forbidden from the D3
contrast score.

## Developmental rule

Across matched fresh situations, ordinary local observations accumulate
role-relative counts for downstream consequence shapes attached to each
anonymous affordance shape. A temporary directional ARROW may form only when:

- exactly one affordance has a recurrent downstream shape meeting the frozen
  support threshold;
- that affordance's repeatability margin exceeds its competitor's;
- the distinction remains after opaque alternatives and fresh occurrences are
  relabeled.

This is predictive stability, not supplied polarity. The learned relation says
only that one live affordance has better recurrent downstream support than the
other. The learner-visible path contains no GOOD/BAD, CORRECT/WRONG,
reward/punishment, expected answer, chosen/unchosen flag, or evaluator ranking.

## Required controls

- same immediate route effects with different downstream stability:
  discriminate;
- different immediate route effects with the same downstream consequence:
  abstain;
- both downstream consequences stable but different: abstain;
- both downstream consequences unstable: abstain;
- swapping anonymous affordance identities moves the direction with the
  consequence history;
- fresh consequence occurrences, occurrence relabeling, allocation/layout
  perturbation, and handle permutation preserve the relational result;
- shuffling downstream consequences destroys stable direction;
- equal consequence magnitude with reversed structural support reverses the
  direction;
- evidence arriving at the immediate-effect tick is rejected;
- removed/stale route invalidates its directional relation;
- all episode-local directional state and memberships are erased;
- persistent state contains only role-relative affordance/consequence shapes
  and counts, never occurrences, handles, selected indices, concrete
  destinations, boundary-role truth, or semantic polarity.
