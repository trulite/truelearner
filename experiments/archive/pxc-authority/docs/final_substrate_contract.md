# Final retained-substrate contract

Status: **post-M8 retained-physics contract**. H0 and I0 subsequently
established that this physics plus the M0--M8 mechanism suite has not yet been
realized as one continuous organism.

This contract freezes the candidate minimal physical substrate. It does not
add an `M9`, reinterpret any M0--M8 result, or merge the separately established
affordance law into the cognitive authority lineage.

## Retained physics

### CELL

- local mutable state;
- persistent physical identity.

### ARROW

- local directional propagation;
- local mutable coupling.

### SPIKE

- fresh transient occurrence.

### Time and dynamics

- local temporal precedence;
- queue- and order-sensitive propagation;
- threshold firing;
- inhibitory closure.

### Plasticity physics

- local structural variation is physically possible;
- local state can strengthen and weaken;
- structures can arise and disappear.

### Environment

- physical activity can enter;
- physical activity can cross outward;
- downstream physical consequences can return.

### Affordance law

- a complete physical state has deterministic execution;
- support integrated before first threshold firing and inhibitory closure can
  alter the realized trajectory;
- equivalent support arriving after closure is behaviorally inert for that
  resolved competition.

The affordance law is established independently by
`deterministic-affordance-causal-window-authoritative`, peeled commit
`ce735bb7b3dab0d17ede176f863257e19c42900a`. It characterizes execution
physics; it does not advance M8 and does not establish usefulness,
generativity, or a probability representation.

## Explicitly absent from the substrate

The retained substrate contains no supplied correspondence, boundary-role,
causal-direction, event-boundary, lifetime-class, plasticity-target,
correct/wrong credit, request/start, finish/answer, action-choice, probability,
or commitment representation. In particular it contains no semantic action
enum, `LEARN_HERE`, TTL, terminal oracle, evaluator-selected emission, chooser,
softmax, temperature, or RNG requirement.

Physical boundary crossing, local firing, and queue quiescence are retained
facts. Their interpretation as initiation, answer emission, or completion is
not retained physics.

## Post-M8 development rule

From post-M8 onward, a capability failure does not authorize adding cognitive
architecture. The first responses are, in order of scientific relevance:

1. more experience;
2. different experience or developmental curriculum;
3. more internal work;
4. more capacity;
5. more learned lifetime;
6. a richer environment.

Reopening architecture requires a separately named research program with
evidence that the fixed substrate cannot express the target. It cannot occur
as an ad-hoc capability patch.

After consolidation, the intended research sequence is SSA1 (learn when
trajectory variation is useful), SSA2 (long structured generative
trajectories), then scaling. Neither SSA1 nor SSA2 is begun by this contract.
