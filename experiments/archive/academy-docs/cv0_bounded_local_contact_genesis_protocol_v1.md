# CV0 bounded local contact genesis protocol v1

Status: frozen before any CV0 organism, evaluator, or executable change.

Parent: SV1 Static-Gate-A negative `a65c445`.

## Question

Can ordinary local variation create fresh contact CELLs as candidate causal
structure, without knowing sign or usefulness, while unsupported contacts
remain bounded and eventually become reclaimable through already-existing
CELL physics?

## Sole candidate proposal form

For one otherwise-eligible local source `P` and target `X`, the proposed
ordinary variation form is:

```text
             C+ --(+1)--> X
            /
P ----------
            \
             C- --(-1)--> X
```

Each candidate relation receives one fresh ordinary CELL contact compartment.
For each sign independently:

```text
P -> C     ordinary Drive, coupling +1
C -> X     ordinary Drive, candidate coupling +1 or -1
```

`C+` and `C-` must be identical in construction, resistance, threshold,
geometry opportunity, delay, phase, generation, lifetime, and proposal
condition except for the sign of `C -> X`. The law creates both alternatives;
it does not choose a sign, inspect activity or consequence, or create an
"inhibitory cell".

The claim under test is only that an ordinary candidate relation may occupy
its own physical contact compartment and signed coupling may vary
symmetrically.

## Frozen gate order

### A — sign symmetry

One opportunity produces `C+` and `C-` under identical physical treatment
except for outgoing coupling sign. Neither sign receives preferred identity,
placement, lifetime, proposal ordering, or consequence access.

### B — no consequence

Without qualified consequence, all four weak structural connections remain
unsupported and locally disappear. Neither contact may mature merely through
creation or traversal.

### C — bounded creation

One proposal opportunity creates exactly two contact CELLs and four weak
ARROWs. The new contacts must not recursively generate contact variants merely
because they exist or fire. Repeated activation while the candidates remain
live must not create another pair for the same opportunity. Record contact and
ARROW proposal counts, peak live weak structure, PhysicalWork, and eventual
reclamation.

### D — orphan cleanup support

Before a runtime candidate is evidence-eligible, audit the already-accepted
CELL physics. When both structural connections belonging to an unsupported
candidate disappear, its unused contact CELL must already have an ordinary
physical path to become non-live and reusable.

If existing CELL physics has no lifetime, deallocation, or reuse path, CV0
stops negative at D. CV0 must not add garbage collection, orphan detection,
reference counting, incoming/outgoing-degree cleanup, CELL expiry, CELL decay,
or a special contact-cell lifetime.

This support gate is allowed to run statically before implementing the
candidate proposal form: constructing contacts that are known to be
irreclaimable would spend no additional scientific evidence.

### E — positive consequence

Both candidates are exercised. Ordinary consequence physically returns only
at `C+`. Only the positive candidate may mature.

### F — negative consequence

Swap the external physical consequence so it returns only at `C-`. Only the
negative candidate may mature.

### G — permutation

Exchange candidate identities, slots, translated positions, proposal order,
and which sign is useful. Selection must follow physical consequence rather
than sign or identity.

### H — both useful / neither useful

Independent consequence at both contacts may mature both. With no qualifying
consequence, neither may mature and both must be reclaimed.

### I — shared-contact control

Deliberately place `+1/-1` alternatives behind one ordinary contact. Reproduce
SV0 co-maturation, proving that any CV0 specificity comes only from physical
contact-compartment granularity.

### J — representation gate

Reference and Production must agree exactly on the frozen ordered physical
history, proposals, Drive and Modulatory deliveries, participation, durable
changes, deallocations, clock, PhysicalWork, final body, pending activity,
natural quiescence, and same-mechanics replay.

## Hard prohibitions

CV0 may not introduce or inspect any equivalent of:

```text
preferred inhibitory sign
winner or selected candidate identity
sign-specific Modulation
reward, error, stability, or oscillation score
special inhibitory-contact construction
semantic contact role
recursive variant generation
orphan/degree-based garbage collection
CELL expiry added as a CV0 rescue
```

## Decision

- **CV0 positive:** every gate passes without adding cleanup physics. Freeze
  CV0, then rerun the frozen SV1 gates unchanged. Do not run RS2 inside CV0.
- **CV0 negative:** stop at the first failed frozen gate. Do not repair it
  inside CV0.

If Gate D is negative, the result identifies missing ordinary CELL lifetime,
not failed inhibitory learning or failed compartmentalized selection.

FD2, ARC, RS2 Gate B, authority, the oracle, and `arch.md` remain unchanged.
