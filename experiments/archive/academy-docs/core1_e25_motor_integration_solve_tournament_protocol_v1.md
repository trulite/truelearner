## Arm G — local signed gating

A cell may carry a default-off local signed-gating property. Academy enables
it only on the already-existing ARC motor cells.

When transmissions from multiple live subdivision contacts reach the same
gated cell in one physical incidence wave with opposing material signs, those
routes are alternatives. The cell's already-present positive subthreshold
activation is the local opportunity being gated. Competition occurs at local
admission and before route participation:

```text
opposing contact transmissions arrive
-> group outgoing material by sign at their common local target
-> unequal absolute material strength wins directly
-> on an exact tie, local positive subthreshold opportunity exists?
-> if yes, tie resolves toward the target's positive threshold surface
-> if no, neither tied alternative is admitted
-> winning-sign arrivals participate and integrate normally
-> losing-sign arrivals do not participate or integrate
```

The target threshold supplies only a local physical direction. The gate may
not inspect an ARC action, context, useful action, babbler identity,
consequence, reward, evaluator order, future input, or E22 state. Without
already-present positive subthreshold activation, exactly tied opposing groups
are not admitted. A materially stronger group wins without an opportunity,
which lets learned physical asymmetry act autonomously. A negative group
stronger in physical material must win; lone-sign incidence remains unchanged.

Because competition precedes participation, only admitted winning routes may
receive participation increments or E22 return paths. Physical transmission
to the gate is not itself declared successful participation. Direct routes,
lone-sign incidence, Modulatory transmission, route formation, delays,
thresholds, activation decay, PQLC, and quiescence remain unchanged.

## Arm W — physical motor integration window

A cell may carry a separate default-off integration-window property. Academy
enables it only on the already-existing ARC motor cells.

During one physical propagation wave, the first subthreshold material
incidence on such a cell opens a payload-free local hold. Later incidence in
that same propagation wave integrates with the held signed activation without
intervening ordinary activation decay. Synchronous incidence still sums
algebraically and threshold evaluation remains unchanged. When the propagation
wave becomes naturally quiescent, any unresolved held activation is cleared.
Firing also clears activation through the existing firing transition.

The window stores no source, arrow, action, context, sign choice, expected
arrival, result, or consequence. It does not reorder packets, split a
synchronous batch, rectify a sign, amplify material, change a delay, or survive
quiescence.

## Arm G+W — composition

Enable both laws on the same existing motor cells without any interaction term
or special combined path. G changes only which opposing subdivision route
physically participates. W changes only the lifetime of the motor's signed
activation within the propagation wave.

## Gate 2 — delayed credit

For each Gate-1-positive arm, run the unchanged five-turn E14 teaching and

Any failure freezes that arm as not sufficient. No repair or seed substitution

## Gate 3 — autonomous policy

After the closing consequence, clone and ordinarily recover the learned
organism. Probe the four taught frames once each with no babble and no support.
Every root must autonomously express `[1,4,2,3]`.

Only an arm passing Gates 1–3 at `8/8` is a solve.

## Interpretation

- G passes: signed gating alone is sufficient.
- W passes: integration persistence alone is sufficient.
- only G+W passes: both motor-local defects are jointly causal.
- all three fail: the combined motor-integration hypothesis is wrong.
- a single arm that reaches Gate 1 but fails credit or autonomy is not a solve.

If both a component arm and G+W pass, old controls and law surface decide the
smaller sufficient candidate; composition earns no necessity claim.

## Controls and rejection screen

- Both candidate properties default off; frozen E14/E16/E22/E24 evaluators and
  candidate-default behavior remain byte-identical.
- G disabled preserves synchronous algebraic cancellation.
- G with only one sign performs no suppression.
- G with exactly tied opposing signs but no already-present local opportunity
  admits neither alternative.
- G admits a physically stronger negative group, proving it does not encode
  the useful sign.
- A G-suppressed arrow receives no participation increment and no E22 return.
- W disabled preserves ordinary one-unit-per-tick activation decay.
- W integrates staggered same-wave positive incidence to threshold.
- W preserves simultaneous `+1 + -1 = 0` and never fires from cancellation.
- W clears unresolved state at natural quiescence and cannot bridge admissions.

  there is no third mechanism.
- No candidate changes route formation, arrow material, delay, cell threshold,
  consequence timing, PQLC magnitude, E22 topology, evaluator inputs, or action
  map.

Reject on action/usefulness inspection, sign hard-coding independent of local
physical strength, packet-order dependence, timeout or cross-admission state,
passive action metadata, evaluator-supplied routes, exactness mismatch,
non-quiescence, unbounded work, or a full-chain claim based only on motor
firing.
