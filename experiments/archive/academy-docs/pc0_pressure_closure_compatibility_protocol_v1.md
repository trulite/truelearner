Status: frozen before runtime or evaluator edits.

## Question

Can continuously relaxing local participation attenuate pressure on durable
structure without pressure consuming the participation needed by a later local
consequence closure?

PC0 is a narrow successor experiment. It does not resume PD2, run ARC, change
authority, or add a new state variable.

## Single candidate law

At each ordinary pressure epoch, after ordinary time relaxation has updated a
live ARROW's participation:

```text
Q = universal participation impulse
p = min(local participation, Q)

pressure reaching durable structure = Q - p
pressure_load += Q - p
durable_loss = pressure_load / Q
pressure_load %= Q
resistance -= durable_loss
```

Pressure does not subtract from or otherwise mutate participation. Only
ordinary relaxation, same-contact traversal, and deallocation may change that
state.

This differs from PD1 only by deleting:

```text
participation -= min(participation, Q)
```

No conditional protection is allowed. Zero, weak, medium, and strong
participation all pass through the same arithmetic law. No field or branch may
refer to expected closure, Modulation timing, eligibility, grace, deadline, or
task horizon.

## PC0 matrix

Run every physical world under Reference and Production mechanics, two fresh
identity roots, pressure phases `0..9`, and exact same-mechanics replay.

### P0 — graded local pressure

Construct weak routes with zero, weak, medium, and strong continuously relaxed
participation at the same pressure epoch.

- effective pressure must be graded monotonically with participation;
- no positive-participation cases may collapse into one Boolean protected
  class;
- pressure must leave exactly the ordinarily relaxed participation present;
- zero participation must receive the complete pressure quantum.

Effective pressure is measured mechanically as durable resistance loss times
`Q` plus the retained fractional `pressure_load`.

### P1 — pressure before consequence

An actual contact traversal occurs immediately before a pressure epoch.
Qualified Modulation arrives immediately after pressure, including multiple
phases at the same tick.

- pressure may attenuate durable structure according to participation;
- remaining participation must still be available to Modulation;
- the contact must strengthen and PQLC must close;
- source firing or event reordering is forbidden.

### P2 — pressure after consequence

Qualified Modulation closes first and pressure occurs next. Durable learning
must remain lawful, bounded, and phase-independent.

### P3 — no consequence

Traversal without Modulation may provisionally attenuate pressure while
participation remains. Ordinary relaxation must eventually remove that
influence and repeated pressure must deallocate the unsupported weak route.

### P4 — wrong-path activity

Activity and renewed participation on a sibling contact must not attenuate
pressure or enable closure on the tested contact.

### P5 — repeated same-contact use

Repeated actual traversal may maintain provisional viability but must not
increase durable resistance. After use stops, relaxation plus pressure must
eventually deallocate the route.
