# FD1 consequence consolidation under local forgetting protocol v1

Status: frozen before runtime or evaluator edits.

Parent: `fd0-phase-free-local-forgetting-ready-v1`.

## Question

Does a qualified physical consequence make a tentative structure persist
longer under FD0 local forgetting, while traversal alone, wrong-path return,
and stale late return do not?

## Frozen separation

```text
Traversal                     changes transient participation only
Qualified Modulation
  x local participation       changes durable resistance
Elapsed physical time         changes local decay load/resistance
Zero resistance               deallocates and invalidates generation
```

No participation value may enter local decay. No traversal may raise
resistance or reset decay load.

## Single candidate law

Retain the existing CPC1/LR-C resistance gain exactly. Add one consequence of
an actual durable structural change:

```text
before = resistance
resistance += existing qualified Modulation gain

if resistance changed:
    local_decay_load = 0
```

Resetting local decay load means the newly changed durable structure begins a
fresh local persistence state. The reset is allowed only when the existing
physical law produces a positive resistance change. Modulation with zero local
participation, wrong-contact Modulation, Drive, traversal, and ordinary time
cannot reset it.

No resistance increment, decay period, participation impulse, relaxation law,
or schedule may be tuned in FD1.

## Matrix

Run every family under Reference and Production mechanics, two fresh identity
roots, creation phases `0..9`, and exact same-mechanics replay.

### C0 — unused proposal

Resistance-one proposal receives neither traversal nor Modulation.

```text
resistance remains 1 until local age 10
age 10 -> deallocate
```

### C1 — repeated use without consequence

The same proposal traverses repeatedly before age 10 but receives no qualified
Modulation.

- participation becomes positive;
- resistance and decay load remain identical to C0 at equal ages;
- death remains local age 10;
- traversal-only durable gain is zero.

### C2 — one qualified consequence

At local age 5, actual traversal leaves participation and qualified Modulation
arrives at the same contact.

- the existing law determines the resistance gain;
- resistance must increase exactly once;
- decay load must change from 5 to 0 only because resistance changed;
- with the current unchanged gain, resistance `1 -> 4` and death occurs at
  local age `45`, forty ticks after consolidation;
- all absolute creation phases give the same age-relative result.

### C3 — same durable state, same future lifetime

Create the same resistance change at multiple absolute phases and through
fresh identities. Once resistance and local decay load match, identical future
time produces identical remaining resistance, death age, work, and stale
generation behavior.

### C4 — wrong-path Modulation

Traverse contact A and deliver Modulation to separate contact B.

- A receives no resistance gain or decay reset;
- B has no participation and receives no gain/reset;
- A dies at ordinary local age 10.

### C5 — late consequence cannot resurrect

Let the resistance-one route die at age 10, then deliver its otherwise lawful
return at age 11.

- no resistance gain, support, or decay reset;
- stale generation remains unresolved;
- no route resurrection or reproposal is attributed to the dead structure.

### C6 — repeated supported consequence

Deliver actual traversal plus qualified consequence at ages 5 and 6.

- each lawful closure uses the unchanged gain;
- expected resistance is `1 -> 4 -> 7`;
- each actual structural change begins a new local decay state;
- after the second consequence, unsupported structure eventually dies at age
  `76`;
- repeated support does not imply immortality.

## Static prohibitions

FD1 may contain no:

- traversal-based resistance gain or decay reset;
- decay read of participation, support, Modulation, capacity, or world state;
- reset caused by zero-gain/wrong-path/late Modulation;
- permanent-memory flag, learned class, expiry time, or task horizon;
- global forgetting epoch, resource-competition rule, ARC fact, or event
  reordering.
