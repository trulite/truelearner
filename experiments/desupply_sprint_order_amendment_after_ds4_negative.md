## Frozen reason for reordering

Persistence/lifetime, plasticity allocation, and consequence/credit determine
whether the organism can learn autonomously:

```text
what persists or decays
where physical change occurs
which ordinary consequences alter later behavior
```

Request/start and finish/output instead concern semantic conventions at a
control boundary:

```text
which incoming activity is treated as a request
when an internal process is initiated
which outgoing activity is treated as an answer
when an internal process is considered finished
```

Postponing those conventions does not preserve a magical `START` or `STOP`
primitive as irreducible substrate. It leaves their supplied semantic
interpretation explicit and temporary while the deeper autonomy targets are
removed first.

The DS4 attempt #1 negative makes this reordering operationally clean. It is
frozen exactly as observed rather than rescued:

```text

DS4 cumulative       postponed
M3                    remains authoritative
```
