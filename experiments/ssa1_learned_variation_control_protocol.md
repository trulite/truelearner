## Research question

> Can the fixed organism learn whether to collapse or preserve competing
> executable futures according to their downstream physical consequences?

randomness, probability, entropy, temperature, or action sampling.

## Physical setup

Every world contains one local event with at least two genuine executable
CELL/ARROW/SPIKE continuations. Each continuation has its own live path and
physically distinct downstream effect. Local supporters are ordinary cells,
arrows, and spikes. Live learned proposal structure may physically supply
support; the harness may not translate a learned scalar into a score or
winner.

Execution is deterministic from complete state. Different observed
frequencies may arise only because complete transient histories differ.
Exact complete-state duplicates must remain exact.

The environment returns ordinary delayed physical activity. Consequence
topology, timing, and recurrence may differ by world, but magnitudes are
matched where a contrast is claimed. The evaluator may classify worlds and
compare outcomes only after execution.

## Preregistered worlds

### A — one continuation consistently wins

One route repeatedly produces a stable recurrent downstream physical
consequence; competitors do not.

Expected signature:

- productive route structure remains live and receives most future local
  plasticity;
- competitor admission becomes sparse;
- the productive route dominates physical realization across varied
  microhistories;
- shuffled learned allocation reverses or destroys the effect.

### B — several continuations have equivalent consequences

At least two routes produce matched recurrent physical consequences with no
lawful differential.

Expected signature:

- the learner abstains from a privileged direction;
- both executable routes remain viable;
- microhistory changes before closure can still realize either route;
- route index, handle, and allocation order do not break the equivalence.

### C — repetition reduces downstream value

Repeated realization of one route changes the subsequent physical consequence
history so that an alternate route becomes differentially supported. No
novelty, boredom, repetition counter, or diversity reward enters the learner.

Expected signature:

- an initially dominant route loses relative physical support after repeated
  use;
- sparse exploration permits an alternate route to regain support;
- more than one route becomes physically realizable again;
- removing the repetition-dependent physical consequence change removes the
  re-emergence.

### D — uncertainty can be resolved by gathering evidence

One executable continuation returns a physical observation that changes which
later continuation has stable downstream consequences. Immediate terminal
routes lack that observation.

Expected signature:

- the evidence-gathering continuation gains learned support only when its
  returned activity lawfully improves later physical consequences;
- an observation with no later effect does not gain the same support;
- after observation, later route use depends on the physically returned
  evidence rather than a supplied hidden world label.

This world must stop as a scientific ambiguity if the frozen organism lacks a
lawful composition path from returned evidence to later executable support.
No new state representation may be introduced to force it.

### E — predictability is exploitable

The environment physically changes its consequence after repeated exposure to
the same continuation. Persistent use of a single route therefore degrades
its own experienced consequence.

Expected signature:

- fixed-route history loses its retention/admission advantage;
- alternate executable structure persists or is reacquired through the
  frozen exploration and lifetime mechanisms;
- the learned physical landscape does not converge to the exploitable fixed
  route;
- a matched non-exploiting environment permits collapse.

## Hard exclusions

Forbidden in organism or linker:

- substrate changes;
- RNG or injected noise;
- `choose`, sampling, softmax, temperature, entropy, or probability state;
- semantic route/action labels;
- novelty, diversity, curiosity, boredom, uncertainty, information-gain, or
  predictability rewards;
- evaluator-selected winners, plastic sites, or eligibility;
- direct scalar-to-winner or scalar-to-precommit-score conversion;
- new cognitive state or a new learning rule.

World names, route indices, and post-execution comparisons may exist only in
the evaluator.

## Controls

At minimum:

- exact complete-state duplicate;
- fresh occurrence, cell, arrow, and route identities;
- route/handle permutation and A/B mirror;
- allocation/layout permutation;
- equal consequence magnitude;
- shuffled learned allocation;
- raw-history swap;
- absent, blocked, stale, and physically removed route;
- absent eligibility and immediate consequence abstention;
- no second executable route;
- two handles for one physical route are not two affordances;
- post-closure support is visible but inert;
- M8 development fingerprint and frozen authority hashes remain exact.

## Development stages

### PROBE

One fresh deterministic seed. Establish the complete executable loop:

```text
physical affordance competition
  -> realized live route
  -> delayed physical consequence
  -> frozen M6 differential
  -> frozen M5 allocation/lifetime
  -> changed live supporter landscape
  -> later physical competition
```

The PROBE may use worlds A and B. Stop at the first missing physical edge.

### MICRO

Two fresh seeds per world. Run all five worlds and minimal matched controls.
Freeze each world outcome independently; the overall classification may not
hide a negative world.

### GATE

Six fresh seeds per world with fresh identities, mirrors, layouts, transient
history schedules, and the full controls above. Exact duplicate execution is
conjunctive.

No definitive matrix is authorized by development readiness. A claim worth
authoritative evidence requires a separate workflow and protocol.

## Development classifications

- **A — full learned variation control:** all worlds A--E and all controls
  pass from the frozen organism.
- **B — consequence-conditioned landscape control:** A, B, C, and E pass but
  D lacks a lawful frozen composition path or fails its claim. D is reported
  independently, not hidden.
- **C — collapse/preservation only:** A and B pass, but adaptive re-emergence
  does not.
- **D — no learned landscape control:** the frozen mechanisms do not
  causally reshape later physical competition.
- **E — scientific ambiguity:** more than one lawful interpretation survives
  or the required physical variables cannot be separated.

No classification authorizes substrate modification or automatic SSA2.

## Stopping rule

Return only when PROBE/MICRO/GATE reaches a frozen classification, or when a
genuine scientific ambiguity/new representation requirement is encountered.
Mechanical linker failures may be repaired by reusing frozen mechanisms and
following the first missing physical edge. SSA2 and scaling remain blocked.
