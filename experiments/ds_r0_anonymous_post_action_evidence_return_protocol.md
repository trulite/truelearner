## Sole question

> Can actual ordinary activity produced after one selected anonymous
> executable route acquire a temporary relation to the just-executed local
> interaction, without consequence meaning, credit, reward, correctness, or
> an evaluator-created consequence packet?

DS-R0 adds no DS1 update, no comparison between action outcomes, no choice
credit, no boundary-role reconstruction, and no cumulative-prefix authority.
It must stop after a format-only evidence-surface bridge has copied an already
formed temporary relation. A separate byte-identical DS1 retry is required to
discover the next dependency.

## Allowed organism-visible evidence

Only these may enter DS-R0:

- a fresh occurrence for the selected route's root injection;
- fresh occurrences emitted by the actual selected route execution;
- local temporal precedence among those occurrences;
- physical propagation relations produced by that execution;
- the already learned E0 event's temporary local relation;
- ordinary local activity/coactivity and plastic state.

The observer may copy a physical propagation occurrence as it happens. It may
not consume the evaluator-normalized effect fingerprint, activation summary,
expected answer, route score, target role, selected-best marker, or truth
filler identity.

## Forbidden semantics and channels

No organism-visible state or decision may contain or derive:

- success, failure, good, bad, correct, wrong, reward, accepted, or rejected;
- which route should have been chosen;
- a boolean or signed consequence;
- an evaluator-created consequence packet;
- an action opcode or interpretation of an opaque handle;
- a stable prior/future occurrence identity, filler identity, destination, or
  episode identifier;
- an outcome comparison across alternatives;
- a call or bridge to frozen DS1 `apply_consequence`;
- economic, pass/fail, scaffold, wave, or level information.

Evaluator-only checks may compare traces after execution, but those values may
not filter, rank, construct, or alter organism-visible relations.

## Outcomes

### R0-A — anonymous return-relation formation

Repeated ordinary execution experience may consolidate a role-relative return
shape containing only local temporal and propagation structure. On a fresh
target execution it must instantiate an episode-local relation whose members
already exist as actual physical activity occurrences:

```text
just-executed interaction occurrence
        --temporary learned relation-->
later activity occurrence
```

Persistent state may contain only role-relative temporal/propagation shape and
support. It may not retain an `OccurrenceId`, opaque handle, route root, E0
member, concrete CELL destination, outcome, or episode membership.

### R0-B — evidence-surface sufficiency

A mechanical one-to-one bridge may copy the already-existing temporary
relation into a DS1-adjacent anonymous evidence surface. Every copied field
must have one exact DS-R0 relation origin. The bridge may not search raw
activity, decide membership, interpret outcome, create a boolean, or call any
DS1 update path.

R0-B means only that anonymous post-action evidence is physically available at
the next composition boundary. It does not mean frozen DS1 consumed or learned
from it.

## Ordered stages and stopping rule

0. exact frozen lineage and stage-6 parent signature;
1. one actual E0 target forms two actual A1 executable roots;
2. byte-identical frozen DS1 chooses and the selected root physically executes;
3. actual execution emits fresh anonymous activity plus local temporal and
   propagation relations without evaluator effect input;
4. repeated support consolidates a role-relative return shape;
5. a fresh target execution forms exactly the valid temporary return relation;
6. format-only evidence bridge copies that relation one-to-one;
7. transfer, ambiguity, negative, leak, lifetime, and cleanup controls pass.

The first false stage is the only collapse; later stages are `BLOCKED`. Passage
through stage 7 yields only **DS-R0 DEVELOPMENT IMPLEMENTATION READY**. It does
not retry DS1 or create M1.
