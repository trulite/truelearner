## One physical trajectory world

Build one connected CELL/ARROW/SPIKE substrate for the complete trajectory.
There is no episode-by-episode route call and no harness-level state decoder.

At every layer, four physical world states exist. Only the state reached from
the preceding physical effect receives source activity. Each state contains two
real executable continuations. A contender requires four learned-support
arrivals and inhibits its competitor when it first fires. Its effect cell is
connected by an ordinary arrow to the next layer's state source.

Use this fixed physical transition relation, where `0/1` are physical sides,
not semantic actions:

| current state | side 0 -> | side 1 -> |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 2 |
| 1 | 3 | 0 |
| 2 | 0 | 3 |
| 3 | 2 | 1 |

Each layer uses fresh CELL/ARROW identities. The graph may recombine at later
layers, but a realized trajectory records every fired state and physical side.

## Transient histories

Permanent cells, arrows, couplings, thresholds, and learned supporter counts
remain identical across histories. Only transient spike arrival timing differs.

For each state/side, three ordinary supporter relays arrive after local source
firing at delays `1, 3, 5`. A fourth learned-support gate requires both local
source activity and a transient spike. One side's gate receives that spike at
local tick `6`, the other's at local tick `7`. The first contender to integrate
four arrivals fires and closes its competitor through frozen inhibition.

The history word determines which **physical side** receives tick `6` at each
layer. It is fixed before execution, contains no state/route feedback, and is
not visible as a bit, label, probability, or action to the organism. Unvisited
states receive the same transient spikes but cannot fire because their local
source contribution is absent.

Use a fixed deterministic history library. History `h` at layer `d` is:

```text
bit(h,d) = bit d of mix64(h + d * 0x9e3779b97f4a7c15)
```

where `mix64` is preregistered in source before evidence. The first six layers
also carry the six low bits of `h` directly, ensuring that histories `0..63`
are physically distinct without an RNG. This is evaluator-created transient
microhistory, not a requested route sequence; route handles and state labels
are absent from the substrate input.

## Conjunctive target

For every main cell:

1. blank ordinary development produces `[4,4]` without direct structure edits;
2. every history yields exactly `depth` state firings and `depth` continuation
   effects, followed by natural queue quiescence;
3. exactly one state and one continuation effect fire per layer;
4. every observed `(state, side, next_state)` belongs to the fixed physical
   transition relation;
5. no unvisited state emits a continuation;
6. exact complete-state replay produces identical fired cells, trace, end state,
   and trajectory;
7. at least half of the tested histories yield distinct physical-side
   trajectories and distinct full execution-trace fingerprints;
8. both physical sides occur at every layer across the history library;
9. trajectory diversity survives fresh identities, reversed allocation order,
   layout changes, and handle permutation;
10. the permanent substrate fingerprint is identical across transient histories.

This target establishes diversity and validity, not usefulness, creativity,
language, or an autonomous preference for diversity.

## Required controls

1. **No second executable route:** ordinary collapsed development must eliminate
   history-dependent trajectory diversity.
2. **Duplicate handles:** two evaluator handles for one physical effect count as
   one affordance and one trajectory.
3. **Same route effect:** relabeling an effect does not create diversity.
4. **Blocked/stale route:** a physically absent continuation cannot win or
   teleport to its next state.
5. **Broken transition:** removing one effect-to-next-state arrow terminates the
   path at that layer; no downstream state may fire.
6. **No transient variation:** identical permanent and transient state is
   duplicate-exact and yields one trajectory.
7. **Handle permutation:** physical trajectory is invariant while evaluator
   handle names permute.
8. **A/B mirror:** mirroring physical timing and topology mirrors the path;
   route index or insertion order cannot be privileged.
9. **Fresh identities/layout:** relations and validity transfer.
10. **Frozen parents:** Frozen Organism v1, SSA1/S2/S3 artifacts, and the
    authoritative SSA0.3 statement remain exact.

## Hard anti-cheat rules

Forbidden:

- RNG, random choice, sampling, categorical action, softmax, temperature, or
  probability state;
- a loop that asks a chooser which route to take at each depth;
- evaluator-selected winner or evaluator-written next state;
- a stored route sequence, generative decoder, grammar decoder, beam search, or
  trajectory lookup table;
- semantic action/state labels supplied to the organism;
- setting `[4,4]`, proposal records, credit, plastic allocation, or lifetime;
- reopening a suppressed route or treating SSA1 as positive;
- modifying Frozen Organism v1 or authoritative SSA0.3 physics.

The evaluator may decode fired physical identities after propagation and check
them against the world relation.

## Development classifications

- **A — conditional long-trajectory generativity:** GATE satisfies every

- **B — bounded composition:** PROBE/MICRO pass but length-32 GATE loses validity,
