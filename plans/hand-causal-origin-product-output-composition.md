# Compose disconnected causal components

## Outcome

Add one opt-in successor to `RecursiveLearnerReturnBearingContinuation` that
preserves ordinary local competition inside each connected physical outcome graph,
while allowing disconnected nearby components to emit together.
Keep the exact one-joint hand unchanged, repair the step-zero two-joint product
break, rerun the full two-joint controls, and run five joints only if two survives.
This is development evidence only; accepted physics remains unchanged.

## Model

Each output already maps to the physical source from which its outcome returns. Live
physical links induce undirected connected components over those sources. Ordinary
choice and fresh-opportunity allocation act separately inside each component, so the
whole choice is the product of disconnected local choices. Missing outcome topology
is treated conservatively as one unknown component. The rest of the return-bearing
choice order is unchanged.

Two narrower proposals were tested and retained as falsified controls. Immediate
origin overlap and completed-path-origin overlap both split the two opposing motors
of one unchanged joint at upper contact, causing simultaneous opposing outputs and
breaking the exact one-joint identity. Outcome-source topology is the smallest
existing structure that groups those genuine alternatives while separating nearby
independent joint modules before any learner exists.

The transformation is representation-invariant: reordering candidates or resident
module registration cannot change the connected partition or the chosen physical
outputs. The new protocol composes the complete one-joint law; it does not replace or
mutate the predecessor variant.

## Invariants

- Only the new opt-in protocol factors local output choice and fresh-opportunity
  allocation by connected outcome-source topology.
- Candidates in one connected outcome component compete exactly as before.
- Candidates in disconnected outcome components cannot suppress one another solely
  because their output positions are nearby.
- No joint, module, position meaning, direction, target, score, evaluator state, or
  stable learner identity enters the partition.
- The predecessor protocol and exact one-joint positions, motor sides, replay,
  quiescence, exhaustion, and cost remain unchanged.
- Two joints must pass identity, registration, proximal perturbation, both limits,
  both escapes, both signs, replay, quiet, and activity-normalized work before five
  joints execute.
- All inherited negative controls and the frozen two-joint failure remain unchanged.
- No accepted default or authority record is changed.

## Controls and evidence

Held-out cases reorder candidates and module registration and perturb only the
proximal component after a shared development prefix. Negative controls keep shared
origins competing, empty origins conservative, the old protocol unchanged, the exact
one-joint source-mapped history unchanged, and five stopped after any two-joint
failure. Evidence retains per-step component positions, cumulative pose, output
sources, drive, foreign drive, returns, writes, reads, choices, topology, work,
comparisons, scans, replay, quiescence, exhaustion, and the first failed arrow.

Killing falsifiers are shared-origin coactivation; disjoint-origin suppression; order
dependence; any predecessor drift; remaining two-joint failure; five-joint failure
after two survives; cross-component corruption after proximal perturbation;
superlinear unexplained work; replay difference; non-quiescence; or exhaustion.

Because the worktree has no clean named pre-benchmark commit, this turn may produce
validated development evidence but must not claim frozen benchmark authority.

## Risks and rollback

Learned physical links could correctly merge components later but could also reveal
an unintended cross-component causal bridge. The permanent component trace, foreign
drive counter, registration control, perturbation, and exact one-joint identity expose
that risk. Component construction adds linear topology work; multi-joint counters and
the warm suite bound it. Rollback removes the new protocol and restores the single
competition component without touching the frozen parent or adapter.
