## Model

The focused receptor product and the palm action product are separate physical
components. `ResearchFocusedActionProjection` is either `Isolated` or
`PalmHorizontal`. `Isolated` is the identity on the established focused body.
`PalmHorizontal` places each existing neutral focused relay at the center of the
palm-horizontal local field and adds the same fixed span from the palm anchor to
the relay and from the relay to both output-specific palm outcomes. The already
admitted receptor firing is not copied. Its one relay fans out inside the body,
so the projection is symmetric under exchanging the two motor directions.

The focused organ retains its projection with its sites and previous receptor
frame in the current research checkpoint. Construction and restore reject a
projection without a focused organ, a focused projection combined with the old
sparse visual effects, or a restore configuration that disagrees with retained
anatomy.

The core physical trace records the sorted distinct physical origins that
actually completed each output candidate path. `ResearchChoiceDiagnostic`
projects those origins without changing selection. The external experiment may
therefore ask whether a focused signal origin appears in a real executable
candidate before comparing cue branches; this observer result never returns to
the organism.

The research world seeds both eyes with the same blank field, saves one focused
body, and restores isolated cue branches with two real illuminated ANSI keys.
Each branch runs through `WorkstationSession`, physical collision, output
integration, and returned outcomes. The complete projection and isolated-relay
control share configuration, cue order, step budget, body initialization,
focused transducer, and evaluator. Exact replay, reversed evaluation order,
normal-body regression, parent focused-body behavior, and semantic firewall are
required.

## Invariants

- One world light change creates at most one admitted input per changed focused
  factor; action projection adds links, not a second input.
- Every projected focused relay connects symmetrically to both opposing
  palm-horizontal outcomes and carries no cue, key, coordinate, target,
  direction, correctness, reward, or evaluator state.
- Removing the projection preserves the established focused body and its exact
  receptor and learner divergence while removing only the visual-to-palm span.
- Candidate origin diagnostics report retained physical lineage exactly and are
  causally inert; they cannot affect path formation, choice, output, or outcome.
- Both cue branches begin from one identical body and device checkpoint, reach
  natural quiescence, and replay exactly.
- A claimed action-participation witness contains a focused physical origin in
  an executable palm-horizontal candidate and a cue-dependent difference at or
  after that candidate.
- Existing learner law, output-specific return, first-choice outcome lifetime,
  hand behavior, default sparse body, focused body checkpoint replay, and
  Academy semantic firewall remain unchanged.

## Controls and evidence

Held-out cases are opposite real illuminated keys, swapped branch evaluation,
unequal eye raster dimensions, a focused change that produces no action, and a
candidate with only non-focused origins. Negative controls are isolated relays,
no visual change, same-cue repeat, default sparse body, invalid projection
without focus, incompatible sparse visual effects, corrupt checkpoint anatomy,
exact replay, accepted hand regression, and semantic firewall. Falsifiers are a
duplicated visual input, asymmetric wiring, selected cue region, hidden desired
direction, missing focused origin at the claimed candidate, action divergence
before receptor divergence, projection-removal equality at every action rung,
replay failure, non-quiescence, parent drift, or a warm suite at or above ten
seconds.

Evidence is one compact artifact with receptor changes, focused-origin candidate
participation, candidates, choices, outputs, movements, fingerprints, work,
memory, replay, controls, and first divergences; plus validated campaign,
candidate, convergence, and verification records. It contains no expected
action or organism-visible evaluator result.

## Risks and rollback

The exact receptor product makes a full anatomical fan-out large. Keep links
bounded and report their cost. A symmetric projection may still produce no
executable action; that is a valid localized failure, not permission to assign
directions to cues. If it fails, preserve the trace and locate the first missing
arrow before another solve. Rollback selects `Isolated`, removes only the added
projection links and origin observer field, and leaves the focused body parent
unchanged.
